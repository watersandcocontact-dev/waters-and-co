# Re-engagement copy templates

Cadence: 3-5 touches over ~2-3 weeks. Most positive replies land on touch 2-3.
Subject lines: 28-50 chars, front-load the hook, a direct question beats cleverness.

## Touch 1 — soft re-open (9-word style)
No pitch, no signature clutter. One question referencing their original ask.
> "[First name] — are you still [looking into / evaluating] [specific thing they inquired about]?"

## Touch 2 — value-add
A proof point or resource relevant to their original use case, or something addressing
the specific objection that stalled the deal. Still no hard pitch.

## Touch 3 — trigger event / new angle
Reference a real change in their world (if known) that reopens the buying window.
If nothing specific is known, skip this touch rather than inventing a trigger.

## Touch 4 — breakup / permission-to-close
Two sentences, no guilt, direct beats cute. Highest-reply-rate message in the sequence
on warm/lapsed lists (much lower on cold lists — don't expect cold-list results here).
> "Should I close out your file, or is this still worth revisiting? Either way's fine —
> just let me know so I stop following up if the timing's wrong."

## Rules that apply to every touch
- Plain text, not designed HTML.
- Single CTA.
- Reference specific past context — the recipient should recognize themselves in the email.
- If a detail isn't in the actual thread history, use a [BRACKET] placeholder, don't guess.
