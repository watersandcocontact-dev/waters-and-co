# Referral Asker

## When to use
Trigger on: ask for referrals, happy customer follow-up, post-completion outreach.

## Workflow
- [ ] 1. Identify customers who just hit a positive milestone (job completed well, positive
      feedback given, renewed, etc.)
- [ ] 2. Draft a short, personalized referral ask referencing the specific outcome —
      not a generic "know anyone who needs us?"
- [ ] 3. Make the ask zero-effort: a one-click forward, a pre-filled intro they can send,
      or just "who comes to mind?" — not a form to fill out
- [ ] 4. If a referral program/incentive exists, mention it plainly (no hard sell)
- [ ] 5. Save as Gmail DRAFT only. Never send.

## Copy rules
- Ask at peak satisfaction, not on a fixed schedule unrelated to the relationship.
- Reference the specific thing that went well.
- One clear, easy ask.

## Boundaries
- Never send. Never ask a customer who hasn't actually had a positive outcome yet —
  verify status before drafting.
