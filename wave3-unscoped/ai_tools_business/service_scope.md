# AI Tools for Business — Service Scope

Practical AI tool setup and training for small businesses — reuses the
trades/SME client base and outreach channels already built for GBP,
ReviewGen, and MissedCall, rather than needing a new audience found from
scratch.

## What's actually delivered

- **Tool selection** — helping a business pick the right AI tool for a real
  problem they have (not "AI for AI's sake") — e.g. an AI note-taker for a
  tradie who hates paperwork, an AI-drafted quote/invoice assistant, a
  simple chatbot for their own website's FAQ, AI-assisted social content
  drafting
- **Setup** — actually configuring the chosen tool for their business:
  accounts, integrations, initial prompts/templates tailored to their
  trade or service
- **Training** — a session (or two) showing the business owner/staff how
  to actually use it day-to-day, not just that it's been switched on
- **Light ongoing support** — troubleshooting and prompt/template
  refinement as an optional add-on tier

## Client base and channel reuse

Same businesses you're already reaching via GBP/ReviewGen/MissedCall
outreach are the natural first audience — a trade business owner who's
already trusted you with their Google profile or missed-call reception is
a warm lead for "want help setting up an AI tool for your quoting/admin
too?" Pitch as a bolt-on during those conversations before running
separate outreach for this line.

## Guided-mode note — competitive discretion, not a compliance issue

**Don't reveal how your own ops hub/backend is built** when discussing this
service with clients — if a client asks how you run your own business
systems, keep it general ("I use a system I built myself to track this
kind of thing") rather than describing the hub's actual architecture,
schema, or that it's a custom Flask app. This is ordinary competitive
discretion (your own tooling is part of what makes your service worth
paying for), not a regulatory or safety boundary like the crypto line's —
just don't hand a client the blueprint to a system that's part of your own
edge.

## What's out of scope

- Building custom software from scratch (this is tool *setup and training*
  on existing AI products, not bespoke development)
- Any advice that strays into legal, financial, or medical territory just
  because the AI tool touches that domain — e.g. helping a client set up
  an AI assistant is fine; advising them on what to actually tell their
  accountant via it is not your call to make
