# Weekly Client Tips — Free Value, No Cannibalisation

**Purpose:** a rotating bank of genuinely useful, zero/near-zero-cost content
to email clients weekly — builds trust and keeps Waters & Co top-of-mind
between paid jobs, without handing away what people already pay for.

**The one hard rule (same as the website copy rule, DECISIONS.md
2026-08-06):** every tip below is either (a) something we don't charge for
anyway, (b) awareness-level only — enough to be genuinely useful, not
enough to replace the paid depth/consistency/done-for-you part — or (c)
adjacent to a paid line, not a substitute for it. If a tip ever reads like
a full how-to for something on the price list, cut it or soften it.

**Compliance guardrails, carried over from CLAUDE.md:** crypto tips stay
mechanics-only (never "should I buy"); Pension/Centrelink tips stay
factual/informational (never personalised financial advice); nothing here
is tax advice.

## A. Free "services" like Lost Super — genuinely free, no cost to us

Things we can actively *offer*, not just mention, funded either by
goodwill (retention/referral value) or an existing referral-fee model,
same shape as Lost Super Navigation.

1. **Lost super search** (already live) — free to client, referral-fee
   funded if it becomes a real TPD claim.
2. **Free concession/rebate eligibility check-in** — we already found
   there's no viable paid market for this (`wave2/concessions/pricing_sheet.md`)
   — perfect to offer free instead of dropping it. A 5-minute "let's see
   what you're entitled to" add-on for any Tech Concierge/Downsizing/
   Pension visit.
3. **Free Google Business Profile "scorecard"** — a quick free audit
   flagging what's missing (categories, photo count, response rate) as a
   teaser for non-clients. Doesn't reveal the ongoing management
   cadence/strategy, just a checklist-style score.
4. **Free scam check-in line** — "got a call or text that felt off? Call
   us, we'll tell you if it's a scam" — for Tech Concierge/Crypto
   Literacy/Pension clients. Minutes of phone time, huge trust payoff with
   an audience that's a common scam target.
5. **Free land-tax deadline reminder** — a once-a-year calendar nudge
   ("valuation notices usually land in [month], objection window is 60
   days in most states") — keeps Land Tax top-of-mind before the paid
   season without doing the actual objection work for free.
6. **Free grant "am I even close?" check** — a fast yes/no on whether a
   specific grant they saw is worth pursuing, not the full curated report
   (that's the $79-99 paid product) — a teaser, not a substitute.

## B. Weekly tip rotation — trades & SME clients

*(GBP, ReviewGen, MissedCall, Land Tax, Bookkeeping, Grant-Finder, AI
lines, Real Estate agents)*

- **Free-resource nudge (monthly slot):** "business.gov.au's grant finder
  is free and worth a quarterly browse" — builds trust (we're not
  gatekeeping), and still funnels interest toward the paid shortlist
  report for anyone who doesn't want to do the digging themselves.
- **Scam/fraud alert:** invoice fraud, fake-supplier-email patterns doing
  the rounds that month — genuinely protective, zero cost to produce
  (a paragraph), nothing to do with any paid line.
- **ATO key-dates reminder:** BAS due dates, super guarantee due dates —
  we don't lodge BAS, so this is pure goodwill and positions us as
  informed/adjacent-helpful without competing with a BAS agent.
- **"One thing to check on your Google listing this week"** — a single,
  specific, low-effort nudge (e.g. "reply to your oldest unanswered
  review") — genuinely actionable, but a one-off nudge isn't the same
  as the consistency/cadence the paid service actually delivers.
- **Land-tax valuation notice reminder** — seasonal, state-specific.
- **Client win of the month** (with permission) — social proof, not
  advice; e.g. "one of our land-tax clients had their valuation reduced
  by $X this year."

## C. Weekly tip rotation — seniors & family clients

*(Tech Concierge, Crypto Literacy, Digital Legacy, Downsizing, Photo
Digitisation, Pension)*

- **Scam of the month** — far and away the highest-value slot for this
  audience. Real, current scam patterns (phone, SMS, email), explained
  plainly. Directly reinforces Tech Concierge/Crypto Literacy positioning
  without doing a paid session's worth of work for free.
- **Concession/rebate reminder** — ties to the free check-in above.
- **Centrelink/Age Pension threshold updates** — factual only, e.g. "the
  asset test threshold was indexed on [date]" — informational, not advice,
  never "here's what you should claim."
- **One free device tip** — e.g. "here's how to block spam calls on an
  iPhone" — a real, complete mini-how-to for something small and generic,
  not connected to any specific paid troubleshooting work.
- **Lost-super awareness reminder** — recurring slot, genuinely free both
  ways.
- **Photo/memory preservation reminder** — seasonal ("old photos and tapes
  degrade a little more every year") — the same honest hook already used
  in the website copy, not a how-to for digitising them yourself.

## Suggested cadence

One list, alternating audience week to week (or split into two lists if
the trade/SME and seniors/family client bases are different enough to
warrant separate sends) — keeps each email short, skimmable, and never
salesy. A simple structure that works for either audience:

1. One-line greeting.
2. The tip (2-4 sentences, genuinely useful on its own).
3. One soft line connecting it to a relevant paid service, only if it
   fits naturally — never forced.
4. Sign-off, no hard call-to-action every time (constant "buy now" energy
   kills goodwill email lists fast).
