# AI Lead-Response for Real Estate Agents — Service Scope

**Status: validated 2026-08-01 as GO WITH CAVEATS — narrow, not premium.**
US/UK/Canada remote-deliverable. Not a novel product — a managed-service
positioning against expensive/complex incumbents, since the underlying
"AI chatbot" layer is already commoditized.

## What's actually delivered

A fully managed, hands-off service (not a tool the agent has to
configure): instant SMS/email response to new leads, database
reactivation of cold leads, and follow-up sequences — for solo/
non-technical agents who won't self-serve a DIY tool or negotiate an
enterprise platform's onboarding.

## Why this is narrow, not a flagship line (read before pitching)

The "AI chatbot for real estate leads" layer is already a commodity:
Roof AI starts near $11/mo, Collect.Chat runs $24-49/mo self-serve, and
Fiverr already has real-estate chatbot builds for ~$45 one-off. The
value here is *managed delivery for agents who won't touch a dashboard*,
not a technology edge — price and pitch accordingly, don't oversell this
as differentiated tech.

## Pricing

**$129-199 AUD/month flat**, positioned explicitly against the expensive/
complex incumbents (Structurely $499+/mo + $2,000-2,500 onboarding;
Ylopo $1,500-3,500+/mo including mandatory ad spend; kvCORE/BoldTrail
$499-999/mo team plans, solo agents effectively priced out) — not against
the $20/mo DIY tier, which this doesn't try to beat on price alone.

## Real demand evidence (not assumed)

r/realtors threads describe an agent paying $3,200 for 60 Zillow leads
and closing zero because competitors responded in 90 seconds; general
consensus that only teams with dedicated instant-response capability make
paid-lead math work, and solo agents mostly can't — that's the actual
gap this line fills. Multiple live Upwork postings for GoHighLevel/n8n
real-estate lead automation confirm agents are already paying for this,
just cheaply and piecemeal.
