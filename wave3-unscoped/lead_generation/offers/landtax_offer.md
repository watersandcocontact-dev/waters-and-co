## The offer

**Problem.** Your land tax or council rates bill is based on a mass
assessment that's often wrong — and most owners have no idea it's
challengeable, or how.

**Promise.** We check whether your valuation is actually inflated
against real comparable sales, prepare the objection evidence and letter,
and you get overpaid tax refunded — with interest, per the relevant
state's process.

**Timeline.** Objection lodged within your window (60 days in most
states, 2 months in VIC, NT is 30 days — see `wave1/landtax/state_*.md`
for the client's specific state). Valuer-General/authority review
typically ~90 days after that.

**Price.** Flat $590 for a standard residential objection (research +
comparable-sales evidence + drafted objection letter — client lodges it
themselves via the state's identity-verified portal, per
`objection_letter_template.md`'s notes). Complex/commercial properties:
from $990.

**Guarantee.** Free 15-minute eligibility check before any fee changes
hands — if the valuation is genuinely defensible and not worth
objecting to, we say so up front rather than take the fee anyway.
