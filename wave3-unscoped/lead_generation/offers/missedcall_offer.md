Format: Martell's Problem/Promise/Timeline/Price/Guarantee. Numbers pulled
from the already-CONFIRMED pricing (`wave1/missedcall/pricing_sheet.md`,

## The offer

**Problem.** You're on the tools. The phone rings out. That job goes to
the next tradie who shows up on Google.

**Promise.** Every missed call gets answered or texted back fast, with
the job details landed straight into your day — you stop losing jobs to
voicemail, and (bundled) your Google listing actually gets found in the
first place.

**Timeline.** Missed-call reception live within 7 days on your existing
number. GBP cleanup live within 14 days.

**Price.**
- Missed-call setup: $349 fixed
- Missed-call ongoing: $199/mo (managed text-back), or from $299/mo for
  full AI reception
- GBP cleanup (bundled): $249 fixed (or $99 for a visibility check first)
- GBP ongoing (bundled): $149/mo
- First-month bundle discount: offer cleanup + first month together at a
  discount to reduce friction, per the existing pricing sheet's own note

**Guarantee.** If the missed-call setup doesn't recover at least one job
in the first 30 days that would otherwise have gone to voicemail, the
setup fee is refunded.

## Advice-call script (call to ask, not to sell)

*"Hey [name], I'm [Owner] — I help trade businesses in Perth stop losing
jobs to missed calls and thin Google profiles. Before I pitch you
anything, can I ask — how do you currently handle calls when you're up a
ladder or mid-job? What actually happens to them?"*

Let them talk. Reflect back what they said. Only then: *"That's exactly
what I built this for — worth a quick look?"*
