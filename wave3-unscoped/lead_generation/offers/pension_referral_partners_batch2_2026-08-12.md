# Pension referral partners — national batch, sourced 2026-08-12

25 verified aged-care financial planning firms across WA/VIC/NSW/ACT/QLD/TAS/SA,
beyond the single RELACS draft already created. Same lane as RELACS: these
firms do Centrelink/DVA *strategy* as billable work but would rather hand off
the claim-lodgement/ARO-review *paperwork* — genuinely complementary, not
competing with their advice. Community legal/welfare-rights orgs are
deliberately excluded from this search — they do the same ARO-review work for
free, so they'd never refer a paying client to a paid alternative (see
`wave2_referral_partners_2026-08-12.md` for that finding).

framed purely as a complementary paperwork resource, no dollar figures.
