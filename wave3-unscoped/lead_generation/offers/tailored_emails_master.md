## The researched approach (full sourcing in DECISIONS.md's 2026-08-07 entry)

Real finding, not assumption: leading with "buy my service" measurably
underperforms. Across three independent large-sample datasets (Hunter.io
11M emails, Woodpecker 20M emails, Interseller 1M+ emails), genuinely
personalized cold emails get **2-3x the reply rate** of generic ones, and
**soft CTAs beat hard CTAs 3x over** (4.2% vs 1.4% reply rate, Puzzle
Inbox, 200K+ emails, same copy — only the CTA changed).

**The structure every email below follows:**
1. **Subject** — short, specific to them, never generic
2. **Observation** — one real, verifiable fact about their business (not flattery)
3. **Value** — something genuinely useful, whether or not they ever reply
4. **Bridge** — one sentence connecting it to what we do, no feature list, no pricing
5. **Soft close** — one easy question, never "book a call now"

Target: 50-120 words, plain text (no images/HTML — reads as human, avoids
spam filters), one clear ask, no urgency language.

---

## How to use this

Each entry has the real source for its "why this angle" line — re-verify
anything before sending if it's been more than a few days (businesses
close, emails change). Send one at a time, not a blast — this is meant to
read as a person reaching out, not a campaign.
