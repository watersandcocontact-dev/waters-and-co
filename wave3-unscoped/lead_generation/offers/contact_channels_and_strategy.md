## The actual recommendation

**Default to text, not a cold call.** A tradie on the tools can't take a
call — that's the exact pain being pitched, so a cold call landing
mid-job gets the same treatment as a missed customer call: ignored or
knocked back on reflex. A short text gets read between jobs, doesn't
need both people free at the same moment, and doesn't put anyone on the
spot. **Call becomes the natural next step once someone replies**, not
the opener — that's when the real advice-conversation from the offer
docs happens.

**Exceptions:**
- **B2B referral-partner contacts** (accountants, buyers' agents for
  land tax) — email is the natural professional opener, not a text.
- **Businesses with no findable mobile, phone-only via a landline, or
  Facebook-only** — see per-business notes below; use whatever channel
  actually reaches them, don't force text where it doesn't fit.

## Text template (tradies, adapt the bracketed part per business)

*"Hi [name], Chris here — I help WA/AU trade businesses stop losing jobs
to missed calls & thin Google profiles. [One-line specific hook, e.g.
'Saw a review mentioning a call got cut short asking for a quote'] — no
sales pitch, just curious if that's a regular thing when you're flat
out? No worries either way."*

Short, specific, easy to ignore without feeling bad, easy to reply to.
If they reply with anything engaged, move to the advice-call script in
the relevant offer doc.

## Email template (referral partners / businesses with a real email only)

Subject: *Referring clients to each other — [Waters & Co / land tax]*

*"Hi [name], I run a land-tax/rates objection service for property
owners in [state] and regularly meet people who'd benefit from a good
[buyers' agent / property accountant] — and you'd likely meet clients
who've had a valuation shock. Worth a short call about referring each
other's clients? No obligation either way."*

## Process for the rest of the list (not individually verified yet)

For every remaining business: check the Google Business Profile first —
most now show a **"Message"** button for businesses that have SMS/chat
enabled, which is the same low-friction channel as a text without
needing to find a personal mobile. If not available, check for a real
website (many were confirmed to exist during the research even where a
direct number wasn't pulled) and use its contact form or listed email.
Call only as a last resort or a follow-up once someone's engaged.
