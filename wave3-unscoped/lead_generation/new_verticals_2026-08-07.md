## Opportunity 1 — Downsizing/estate-clearance referral partners for Digital Legacy + Photo Digitisation (Perth)

**Why this instead of the literal brief.** Direct research into Perth
aged-care facilities, retirement villages, and seniors' community groups
(U3A branches, Probus, Men's Sheds, library digital-literacy programs)
came up genuinely thin against the verification bar:
- The one specific "seeking a tech volunteer" lead found
  (Wheller on the Park's "Tech Guru Volunteer" role) turned out to be in
  **Chermside, Queensland — not Perth**. Caught and discarded rather than
  used.
- Bethanie and Juniper (WA's two major retirement-village operators)
  don't publish individual village lifestyle-coordinator emails anywhere
  public — only a central 1300 number.
- U3A Perth's ~12 branches are run entirely by volunteers with no
  published branch-level contact emails found; Probus WA's only public
  email (`probusperth@gmail.com`) is a generic membership inbox with no
  specific pain signal attached.
- TRY Computing and Perth Computer Coaching are **competitors** in this
  space (volunteer/paid computer tutoring), not referral partners.

This matches what `global_expansion_research.md` already concluded for
this cluster: "no dedicated marketplace exists for senior-tech-help,
digital-legacy... this is white space, not a channel" — it needs
direct/relationship outreach and probably an in-person visit to build,
not a one-session desk-research contact list.

**The workable pivot**: Perth downsizing, decluttering, and deceased-estate
clearance businesses. They're hired by the same client (a senior or their
family, moving house or handling an estate) for the physical side of
exactly the same job Photo Digitisation and Digital Legacy solve the
*information* side of — sorting boxes of old photos, documents, and
sometimes a deceased relative's online accounts. Every one below is
verified to explicitly handle physical downsizing/clearance work and
explicitly does **not** mention any digitising/digital-accounts service —
a clean, complementary referral gap, not a competing pitch.
