# Global Expansion Research — English-Speaking Markets (2026-08-01)

Scope decision: only the 8 genuinely location-independent business lines were
researched for international demand. Excluded and why:

- **LandTax, Bookkeeping, Concession, GrantFinder, Pension, LostSuper** — tied
  to Australian government/tax/super systems specifically. "Remote delivery"
  doesn't make these portable; the subject matter itself is Australian.
- **Downsizing, Photo Digitisation** — genuinely require physical presence.
- Markets scoped to **US, UK, Canada, NZ, Ireland** (English-speaking) —
  Chris can't deliver a consultation in a language he doesn't speak, so
  "worldwide" was narrowed to where the service is actually deliverable.

## Real, live demand found (verified, not inferred)

**Video/Podcast Repurposing — strongest signal of the whole batch.** Real,
currently-open Upwork postings with actual budgets: a long-term podcast
producer role ($400 fixed), a video editor role ($500 fixed, contract-to-hire),
an ongoing podcast editor role (hourly, 30+hrs/wk, 6+ months), plus several
smaller one-offs ($25-30). Budgets cluster $100-500/episode — matches the
existing $150-400 pricing exactly. Retainer-style postings outnumber one-offs,
meaning recurring revenue is realistic here, not just one-off gigs.

**GBP/Local SEO — 319 live Upwork jobs, 94 live AI-reception jobs.** Real
postings found with budgets: "SEO + GBP Blog Posting Specialist" $600 fixed/mo
(US clinic), "Local SEO Expert" $300 fixed, "Google Review Crisis Specialist"
$8-20/hr. On missed-call reception: a competitor is already selling "Sofia"
(an AI voice agent) to US HVAC shops for $500 fixed + commission — proof the
model works commercially in the US right now, not just in Australia.

**AI Tools for Business — weak as a marketplace category.** Most AI-tagged
Upwork jobs are dev/automation build work, not owner coaching/training. Real
pain signal exists (owners "freeze at the starting line," tool-choice
paralysis is a repeated pattern in secondary sources) but it doesn't convert
to budgeted job postings — this line needs relationship-based selling
internationally, not a marketplace listing.

**Crypto Literacy / Digital Legacy — underserved, but thin direct-demand
evidence.** No established remote/video competitor found for either
specifically (general tech concierge is crowded, these two aren't). But
Upwork/Fiverr search couldn't confirm real buyer demand (search-engine
indexing can't reach Upwork's live job feed properly — a data-access
limitation, not proof of no demand). Fiverr has generic password-manager
gigs ($40-100) but nothing packaged as senior-focused "digital legacy."

**Tech Concierge (remote) — real price anchor already exists.** Candoo Tech
(US) runs the closest direct comparable: $75/hr 1:1 video sessions, sitting
right in Chris's existing $60-80 target band — not a vacuum, a validated
price point. Cyber-Seniors (US/Canada) offers free volunteer tech help,
which could suppress willingness-to-pay for basic troubleshooting but is
inconsistent/no-guarantee, leaving room for a paid reliable alternative.
**UK looks like the most open lane of the three markets** — no national
brand found, market is fragmented small local operators mostly focused on
in-person visits.

## Practical constraints that actually change the plan

1. **Timezone is a real, not theoretical, constraint.** Perth (AWST,
   UTC+8, no DST) has a dead zone roughly 1-4pm for every English-speaking
   market — no viable live-call window at all. North America overlaps with
   Perth mornings (their evenings); UK/Ireland overlaps with Perth evenings
   (their daytime). A single operator could realistically serve North
   America in the morning and UK/Ireland in the evening, but that's a long
   split day, not a normal 9-5 — this is the single biggest practical
   limiter on the live/synchronous services (Tech Concierge, Digital
   Legacy), much less relevant to the async ones (GBP, Video Repurposing,
   AI reception's daily log review).
2. **Dialzara (the AU-recommended AI phone platform) confirmed for US/UK/
   Canada/Australia — NOT confirmed for NZ or Ireland.** Check directly
   with the platform before selling into those two specifically.
3. **Stripe international payments are a non-issue** — USD/GBP/CAD all
   settle fine to AUD (~2% FX fee, or swap for a 1% payout fee by enabling
   USD/NZD settlement). Routine, not a blocker.
4. **Website international SEO is premature right now** — don't build
   country-specific landing pages speculatively. Wait for analytics to
   show organic non-AU traffic actually landing on the site first, and let
   real signal (not ambition) pick the first target country.
5. **Crypto education regulatory posture is greyer abroad than in Australia.**
   US: SEC/FINRA's crypto-asset classification is still being actively
   finalized as of 2026 — more unsettled than ASIC's framework. UK: the FCA
   brought cryptoasset promotions into its financial-promotion regime in
   Oct 2023 with active "finfluencer" enforcement — pure mechanics-only
   education likely sits outside that regime, but content drift toward
   platform/asset endorsement is riskier there than in Australia. Not legal
   advice — verify locally before actually operating in either market.

## Recommendation — priority order, based on actual evidence strength

1. **Video Repurposing** — strongest real demand signal, budgets match
   existing pricing exactly, list on Upwork now.
2. **GBP/MissedCall bundle** — real demand confirmed, but crowded at the
   top end in the US; UK entry tier and the US sub-$500/mo tier are the
   better openings. A competitor already proves the missed-call model works
   commercially in the US.
3. **Tech Concierge (UK specifically)** — genuinely open lane, no national
   competitor found, but constrained hard by the timezone overlap above.
4. **Crypto Literacy / Digital Legacy** — underserved but unproven demand
   via marketplaces; pursue via direct outreach/content, not a marketplace
   listing, and only after the above three have some traction.
5. **AI Tools for Business** — weakest marketplace signal of the six;
   needs relationship-based selling, not a listing — deprioritize until
   there's spare capacity to do outreach rather than list-and-wait.
