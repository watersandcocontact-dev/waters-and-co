### Tier 5 — Hardest: LandTax direct demand (no direct leads found, only where to watch)
No individual prospects found or ethically identifiable (can't target "who has a
high valuation"). Real signal exists but requires monitoring, not cold outreach:
- PropertyChat threads (WA land-tax/valuation tag archive) and Whirlpool Real
  Estate subforum — periodic manual check, not a one-time list.
- WA Landgate's Date of Valuation is **1 August annually**, but the lag before that
  valuation actually reaches a rates/land-tax notice is ~23 months (metro) / ~11
  months (regional) — demand spikes when individual council/RevenueWA notices land,
  not on one national date. Watch for notice season, not a fixed calendar date.
- Facebook "Perth Property Investment" group and "Aus Property Investors" — content
  not publicly readable without joining.
