### Source 1 — Dan Martell, "one-person AI business" video (transcript, as provided)

Six-step method: (1) stop throwing bodies at problems — design the system
that does the work, don't manage headcount; (2) find a painful,
must-have (not nice-to-have) problem in a growing market — pick the
market first, then call 10 prospects *to ask for advice, not to sell*
("if you call to sell, you get advice; if you call for advice, you get a
sale"); (3) solve the problem manually first, by hand, with simple tools
(a spreadsheet, a VA) to get paid while learning the real workflow before
automating anything — then write a one-page offer (Problem / Promise /
Timeline / Price / Guarantee) and sell it to the same 10 people as an
"early adopter" offer; (4) build a clickable, non-functional prototype
("Wizard of Oz") to validate the flow with 5 more customers before
writing real code; (5) build a minimal MVP — core features only, defer
custom requests unless they'd help 80% of users; (6) scale by stacking AI
agents and automated workflows, not by hiring, looping yourself in only
where judgment is needed. Cites his own portfolio company at "$83k/month
recurring revenue with the founder and two part-time contractors" as the
model — flagged as unverified promotional claim in Part 4 of the report
below, not treated as fact.

### Source 2 — Full applied report: "Waters & Co: Applying the Dan Martell
'Sell-Before-Build' Playbook — Client Acquisition for Existing Lines and a
Grounded Scan of New Opportunities"

**TL;DR** — The Martell method fits as a sales discipline: stop treating
the 18 lines as a menu, lead-generate on the 5-6 sharpest-pain lines
(trades missed-call/reception, NDIS provider compliance, land-tax
objections, grant writing, Airbnb co-hosting, AI implementation for SMEs),
each packaged as a one-page Problem/Promise/Timeline/Price/Guarantee offer,
pre-sold via advice calls before further build. The "one-person AI
business" trend is real at the low end (Chatbase US$64k/mo MRR with 2
hires; Pieter Levels ~US$3M/yr solo; Brett Williams/DesignJoy ~US$1.3-2M/yr
solo) and overstated at the high end — Martell's own "$83k/month + two
contractors" company is unverifiable, and his "Fixer $1M→$10M in five
months" example is actually VC-funded Fyxer AI's ~9x ARR growth over
roughly a full year (raised a US$10M Series A in March 2025 — not a lean
solo shop). Best new opportunity: AI implementation for SMEs, entering at
the productized-build floor (A$2,500-$15,000/project + $500-$2,000/mo
retainers), between DIY no-code and $50k+ Big-4/boutique engagements.

**Key findings** — (1) Client acquisition, not service count, is the
binding constraint — Waters & Co already has delivery infrastructure
(ops hub, AI intake, queue); the playbook's value is the front-end:
find the bleeding-neck problem, talk to 10 people for advice, write the
one-page offer, pre-sell early adopters, deliver manually, then automate.
(2) Six lines score "must-have": missed-call reception (~$800-$1,300 per
lost job, per Lucra AI/Skedy), NDIS provider compliance (regulatory,
deadline-driven, registration now expanding the market), land-tax
objection (a pure-upside refund proposition), grant writing (binary
high-value outcome), Airbnb co-hosting (recurring % of revenue), AI
implementation for SMEs. (3) AU pricing benchmarks gathered across all of
these (tradie SEO A$300-$3,500/mo; AI reception A$197-$2,000/mo; NDIS
policy suites A$2,000-$5,000; grant writing A$50-$300+/hr; Airbnb mgmt
15-25% of revenue; AI consulting A$150-$400/hr or A$10k-$80k/project).
(4) One-person AI economics are real at the floor, hype at the ceiling —
64% of agencies charge under $1,000/mo (SE Ranking, 260 agencies);
AI-service churn 50-70% in 90 days vs 5-10% normal SaaS (Prospeo);
average agency net margin ~13% (Promethean Research); Gartner predicts
over 40% of agentic-AI projects cancelled by end of 2027 (press release,
Sydney, 25 June 2025); MIT Project NANDA found 95% of enterprise GenAI
pilots show no measurable P&L impact.

**Part 1 — one-page offers for existing lines** (Problem/Promise/
Timeline/Price/Guarantee format, each with a prospecting channel and an
advice-call opener):

- *Missed-call reception (trades)* — Problem: phone rings out on the
  tools, job goes to the next tradie on Google. Promise: every missed call
  answered/texted back within 60 seconds, job details to inbox, stop
  losing $800-$1,300 jobs. Timeline: live in 7 days on the existing
  number. Price: $290/mo + $490 setup. Guarantee: setup fee refunded if
  no recovered job in 30 days. Prospecting: trade Facebook groups,
  supplier trade desks, sign-written utes. Opener: "I'm building a simple
  missed-call tool for one-man trade businesses — can I ask how you
  currently handle calls when you're up a ladder?"
- *GBP/local SEO (trades)* — Problem: invisible on Google Maps.
  Promise: top-3 map-pack visibility + owned inbound leads. Timeline:
  live in 14 days, ranking movement month 2-3. Price: $450/mo, no
  lock-in. Guarantee: 5 genuine leads in 90 days or free work until you
  get them. Opener: "Where do your jobs come from right now — word of
  mouth, HiPages, Google?"
- *NDIS provider compliance/audit-prep* — Problem: audit looming,
  policies/evidence/staff not ready. Promise: mapped evidence index, mock
  audit, corrective-action plan for zero major non-conformities.
  Timeline: audit-ready in 4-6 weeks. Price: $3,500 fixed + optional
  $250/mo retainer. Guarantee: free remediation of any major
  non-conformity on a prepared scope item. Prospecting: LinkedIn, NDIS
  provider FB groups, NDIS Commission public register. Opener: "What was
  your last audit actually like? Where did you feel exposed?"
- *Land tax/rates objection* — Problem: inflated mass-assessment
  valuation. Promise: assess over-valuation, prepare objection, refund
  with interest if successful. Timeline: lodged within the 60-day window,
  Valuer-General review ~90 days. Price: $550 flat prep fee (valuation
  report, if needed, passed through separately). Guarantee: told upfront,
  free, if not worth objecting. Prospecting: investment-property FB
  groups, property meetups, accountants/buyers' agents as referral
  partners. Opener: "Did your last assessment jump? Would you know how to
  challenge it if it had?"
- *Grant writing (nonprofits)* — Problem: no one on the team has time/
  expertise to write a winning application. Promise: submission-ready,
  criteria-aligned application. Timeline: draft in 2 weeks, final 1 week
  before deadline. Price: $1,500 flat/application. Guarantee: one full
  revision included; no charge if not submission-ready by deadline. Note:
  no commission/success-fee (AFP/GPA prohibit it; can cause awards to be
  revoked) — flat/hourly only. Prospecting: council grant mailing lists,
  GrantConnect, LinkedIn nonprofit board members.
- *Airbnb co-hosting* — Problem: underbooked short-stay eating evenings/
  weekends. Promise: full guest management, dynamic pricing,
  multi-platform listing. Timeline: live in 7 days. Price: 18% of net
  booking revenue (15% no-frills to 22-25% premium is the market range).
  Guarantee: first month at 12%, free if occupancy doesn't improve.
  Compliance note as given in the source: "a co-host who isn't a licensed
  real estate agent must not manage payments outside the Airbnb system" —
  see reconciliation section above for why this line is held regardless.
