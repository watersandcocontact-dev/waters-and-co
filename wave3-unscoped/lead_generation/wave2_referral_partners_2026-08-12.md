## Pension — genuinely new lane, referral-partner model instead of cold-email

Zero leads or referral contacts existed for this line before today. Before
drafting anything, checked live whether the paid **$249 ARO review
assistance** service has free competition — it does, and materially:

- Internal Centrelink reviews, ARO reviews, and appeals to the Administrative
  Review Tribunal (ART, successor to the AAT) **cost nothing to lodge.**
- Free, specialist **welfare-rights advocacy** already exists and is well
  established for exactly this: community legal centres, the Economic
  Justice Australia network, and financial counsellors reachable via the
  National Debt Helpline.

This doesn't kill the $249 service — same logic as paid tax agents coexisting
with free ATO help: speed, a single point of contact, no means-tested intake
queue. But it does rule out community legal/welfare-rights orgs as referral
partners; they'd never send a paying client to a paid alternative to their
own free service.

**Pivoted to a different, real lane: paid aged-care financial planning
firms.** They do Centrelink/DVA *strategy* advice as billable work, but the
actual claim-lodgement/ARO-review *paperwork* is exactly the kind of
low-value-per-hour task a CFP would rather hand off — genuinely
complementary, not competitive, and the referral fits without needing a
financial-advice licence conversation.
