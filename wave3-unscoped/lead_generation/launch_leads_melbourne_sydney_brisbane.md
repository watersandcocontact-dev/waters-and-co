# Launch Leads — Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane — 2026-08-01

Extension of `launch_leads_and_contacts.md` (Perth) using the same
methodology: real, named, verifiable trade businesses with documented
pain — a bad review about missed/rushed calls, a thin or unclaimed
Google Business Profile, or a live job ad for admin/reception work
(proof of budget for exactly this problem). All desk research, checked
2026-08-01 — re-verify each listing immediately before outreach,
profiles and job ads change.

## Competitor pricing check — still undercuts in the bigger, pricier cities

- **GBP/local SEO**: nationally, freelancers run $500-2,000/mo, agencies
  $1,000-5,000+/mo (themepress.com.au, paramark.com.au, waypointdigital
  .com.au, all 2026). Waters & Co's confirmed $99/$249 fixed one-off /
  $149/mo sits well below even the freelancer floor everywhere checked.
- **AI reception/missed-call**: typical AU market $199-699/mo once
  minutes/setup/integrations included, budget end $48-99/mo (Office HQ,
  Hey Jodie — amily.ai, yesai.au, valory.com.au, all 2026). Waters & Co's
  confirmed $349 setup / $199-299+/mo remains competitive against
  both ends.
- No city-specific premium found that would change this — the earlier
  CONFIRMED national pricing pass holds for Melbourne/Sydney/Brisbane too.

   4-year-old review reports a 1,300m² driveway job that got **no quote

   high-value lead.

   "Quotes & Invoicing Administrator," $30-40/hr — already paying to
   solve exactly this problem manually.
3. **Sutton Security & Electrics** (Sunbury) — live SEEK ad, "Office
   Assistant/Manager," $32-40/hr, family-owned 30+ years, single
   decision-maker.
4. **Local Werribee Plumber** (Werribee) — 1.7★/15 reviews; three
   separate reviewers describe no-call/no-show and being ignored across
   5 emails. Strong evidence but a badly damaged reputation may make
   this a harder budget conversation.
5. **Beachside Plumbing Services** (Braeside) — live SEEK ad, "Office
   Administration," $60-80k/yr, but already 4.3★/125 reviews — less
   urgency, still a live budget signal.
6. **Webb Plumbing Services** (Elsternwick) — live SEEK ad, "Operations
   Coordinator," $75-85k/yr + bonus — hiring at a senior level suggests
   they may be solving this internally rather than outsourcing.
7. **Melbourne Gas & Air** (Mernda) — 4.8★/116 reviews overall but one
   buried complaint (no-show, then charged $250, no follow-up response)
   — business looks healthy, owner likely feels no urgency.

there, same limitation as the original Perth research. Every listing
above should be re-verified (phone number, trading status, review count)
immediately before contact — these are live business details that
change.
