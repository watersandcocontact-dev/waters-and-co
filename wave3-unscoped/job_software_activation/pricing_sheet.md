# Job-Software Activation — Pricing Sheet

Fixed-fee configuration of the automations already bundled in a trade
business's ServiceM8 plan — quote follow-up, booking reminders, ETA texts,
invoice chasing — plus optional monthly upkeep. Best margin is the
switch-on sold to an existing GBP/ReviewGen/MissedCall client, where there
is no acquisition cost and the business is already understood.

## Proposed pricing (drafted 2026-08-10 — NOT yet validated by delivery)

| Package | Price | Time | Effective $/hr |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free 15-minute automation check** — which automations are on, which are off, what that's costing | **$0** | ~15min | lead magnet, not a service |
| **Switch-On** — existing ServiceM8 account: quote follow-up, booking reminders, ETA texts, invoice chasing, all message wording rewritten, 30min training, written handover | **$390–490** | ~3.5–4hr | **$98–140/hr** |
| **Full Setup** — new or unused account: job types, quote/invoice templates, enquiry form, accounting integration, everything in Switch-On, 2 training sessions, 14 days support | **$890–1,190** | ~8–10hr | **$89–149/hr** |
| **Switch-On for an existing GBP/ReviewGen/MissedCall client** | **$290–350** | ~2.5–3hr | **$97–140/hr** — best margin, business already understood |
| **Monthly upkeep** — message wording reviewed, new automations enabled as the business changes, quarterly numbers pulled | **$99–149/mo** | ~30–45min/mo | **$132–298/hr** |
| Data migration from another system | quote only | — | out of scope, see service_scope.md |

**Suggested `RATE_CARD` entry if it becomes a line: $115/hr** (blended
across Switch-On and Full Setup; the monthly upkeep row is the same shape
as GBP/MissedCall `management` and would need its own task-type figure
nearer $200).

### The rebate is the pricing story, and it should be quoted honestly

ServiceM8 matches what a client pays a certified Partner for setup and
training with an account credit **up to $2,000**, applied at **50% of the
client's plan base price per month** until the credit is used up.

That makes the correct pitch "this costs you nothing you weren't already
spending" — but only if it is stated accurately:

| Client's plan | Credit applied per month | $490 Switch-On recovered in |
|---|---|---|
| Starter ($29/mo) | $14.50 | ~34 months |
| Growing ($79/mo) | $39.50 | ~13 months |
| Premium ($149/mo) | $74.50 | ~7 months |

**It is a slow-burn subscription discount, not a refund.** Saying "and
ServiceM8 pays you back" without the drip rate would be the kind of
overclaim this business flags in other people's marketing. Say: *the fee
comes back to you as a subscription credit over the next year or so.*

### Second revenue stream, deliberately not priced in

Partners earn **$500 referral bonuses** and **up to 20% ongoing revenue
share** on clients they set up. On a $79/mo plan that is roughly $16/mo
per client — real, compounding, and requiring no delivery time. It is
excluded from the $/hr column above on purpose: it is unearned until the
partner agreement is actually signed and read, and the service should
stand up on its own fee without it.

## Reasoning

- **$390–490 for a Switch-On sits deliberately under a third of the
  incumbent's $1,250.** Not because the work is worth a third, but because
  Growth Local's package includes account creation, templates and 14 days
  of support that a business already running ServiceM8 does not need. The
  Full Setup row is the like-for-like comparison, and at $890–1,190 it
  undercuts $1,250 modestly rather than dramatically — undercutting a
  certified partner heavily on identical scope would invite the obvious
  question about quality.
- **The monthly upkeep is priced well below Growth Local's $197–297/mo**
  because it is genuinely less: no dedicated account manager, no
  business-hours SLA. Pricing it near theirs would be selling a smaller
  thing at the same price.
- **The existing-client rate is where the real advantage is.** Growth
  Local carries customer-acquisition cost in every quote. A Switch-On sold
  to a current GBP client carries none, which is why $290–350 still clears
  ~$100/hr.
- **The free 15-minute check is a lead magnet, not generosity** — it is
  the same wedge as GBP's $99 visibility check and the AI Search
  Visibility free snapshot, and it surfaces MissedCall and ReviewGen
  problems in the same conversation.
- **Not priced against the $26–29/mo software.** That is the client's own
  subscription, not a competitor to this service, and conflating the two
  would invite the "why pay you $490 when the app is $29" objection
  needlessly. The answer, if it comes anyway: the $29 buys the features;
  it does not turn them on or write what they say.

## Confidence and gaps

- **Vendor and competitor pricing: high confidence** — figures fetched
  directly from servicem8.com and growthlocal.com.au, not aggregators.
- **Rebate mechanics: medium-high confidence** — the $2,000 cap and the
  50%-of-plan-base drip rate come from ServiceM8's own rebate material,
  but the exact eligibility conditions (what counts as qualifying setup,
  whether both parties must be on particular plans) have not been read in
  full. Read the terms before quoting the rebate to a client.
- **Partner joining cost: unverified.** No fee is stated on the partner
  page. Absence of a stated fee is not confirmation there is none.
- **Time estimates: low confidence.** The 3.5–4hr and 8–10hr figures are
  estimates, not measured — nothing has been delivered. The $/hr column is
  provisional and should be re-derived after the first 3–5 real jobs, the
  same way Downsizing and Digital Legacy were.
- **ServiceM8 only.** Tradify, simPRO, AroFlo and Fergus were not
  researched. Do not quote them.
