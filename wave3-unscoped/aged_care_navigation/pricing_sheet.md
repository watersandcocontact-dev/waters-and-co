# Aged Care Navigation — Pricing Sheet

My Aged Care registration, assessment preparation, support-person
attendance, application lodgement, provider shortlist. **Paperwork and
preparation only** — no placement broking, no financial advice (see
`service_scope.md`, Boundaries).

Priced as a scope extension of the **Pension** line ($175/hr rate card),
not as a separate line. Bolt-on to an existing Tech Concierge or
Downsizing visit is the highest-margin way to sell it — no second trip.

## Reasoning

**The bundle undercuts ApplyWise's $595 while adding what they can't:
in-person, in-home, in Perth.** ApplyWise is the only competitor found
selling this exact slice at a published price, so it is the anchor. Coming
in at $449–549 is a real discount rather than a token one, and still lands
above the Pension line's $175/hr floor on every row.

The entry tier is deliberately $249–299 — close to the Pension line's own
$249 ARO-review anchor, and low enough that an adult child in crisis says
yes without a family conference about it.

**The free-to-family competitors are not the threat they look like.** They
are commission-funded by residential aged care homes, which means they earn
nothing on the home-care/assessment stage where the ~107,000-person backlog
actually is. They also cannot honestly present a provider comparison, since
their revenue depends on the placement. Undercutting free is impossible;
being the one that isn't paid by the provider is the answer, and it is the
same "no commissions" position RELACS already markets on.

**Where this could be wrong:** nobody has paid these prices yet, the
time-on-tools estimates are guesses, and the two headline competitor
figures were not fetched from source. The assessment-prep hour is the part
most likely to run long on a real job.
