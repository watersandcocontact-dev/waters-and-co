## Per-episode pricing

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | $300-400 | 5 short clips (captioned), show notes |
| Standard | $500-600 | 8-10 short clips, show notes, 1 long-form cut |
| Premium | $700-800 | Standard + custom thumbnails, priority turnaround (48hr), brand-styled captions |

## Notes

- **Service area: Australia-wide** — fully remote, file-transfer based.
- This is the **lowest $/hr service in the portfolio** per `PRICING.md` —
  worth quoting a **monthly retainer** for recurring podcast/video clients
  (e.g. weekly episode = 4/month) rather than one-off pricing, since
  editing speed improves fast once you know a client's format, and a
  retainer smooths the workload.
- Consider a first-episode discount or free sample clip to prove quality
  before a client commits to an ongoing arrangement — low cost to you
  (an hour or two), strong conversion lever for a content-quality service
  people can literally see before buying.
