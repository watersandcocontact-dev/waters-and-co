# AI Implementation for SMEs — Pricing Sheet

**Sourced from real competitor data gathered 2026-08-01** — live Fiverr/
Upwork listings weighted over vendor-blog "starting from" marketing pages
(most AU "AI consulting cost" search results are published by competing
AI-automation shops price-anchoring their own market — treated as
upper-bound marketing, not independent data).

## Tier 1 — Single Automation Build

**$990–1,490 AUD fixed.** One clearly-scoped workflow (e.g. "leads from
your website/inbox auto-logged into a simple CRM with confirmation emails
and payment requests" — literally what was built for Waters & Co's own
hub). One discovery call, one revision round, 1-2 week delivery.

## Tier 2 — Small Business System

**$2,500–4,000 AUD fixed.** Multi-step system (lead capture + CRM +
invoicing/payments + basic reporting) — 50-70% below the typical
$5k-15k boutique SME project.

## Optional light retainer

**$150–250/month** — monitoring and small tweaks, priced near actual
marginal cost (maintaining a working Claude-Code-built system is cheap
once built) rather than the $500-2,000/mo boutique-agency retainer norm.

## Honest note on delivery cost

Tooling/compute genuinely is cheap (solo operators self-report
$80-500/mo even servicing 10-20 clients). The real cost is Chris's time
in discovery/scoping/testing against a non-technical client's messy real
data — bounded at roughly 3-8 hours per client, not zero. Price the
tiers to cover that time; this isn't a "near-infinite margin" line, it's
a genuinely cheaper cost structure than an agency's staff overhead, which
is a different (more honest) claim.
