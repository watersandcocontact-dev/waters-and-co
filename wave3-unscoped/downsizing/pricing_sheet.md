# Senior Downsizing/Cleanout — Pricing Sheet (draft, adjustable)

Based on `PRICING.md` row 18 ($50-80/hr or project flat fee, ~4-6hrs/job day).

## Pricing options

| Model | Price | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Hourly | $50-80/hr | Smaller jobs, unclear scope on first visit |
| Day rate | $350-500/day (4-6hrs) | Standard single-room or small-property cleanout |
| Project flat fee | Quote after walkthrough — typically $800-2,500 for a full-property downsize | Larger jobs, gives the client budget certainty |

## Notes

- **Service area: local only** — this is the one service in the whole
  portfolio that genuinely requires being on-site.
- Always do a **free walkthrough quote** before committing to a flat fee —
  scope varies enormously between "declutter one room" and "clear an entire
  house before settlement," and a flat quote without seeing the property
  first is guesswork.
- Third-party costs (skip bin hire, removalist fees) should be **itemized
  and passed through separately**, not buried in your fee — same principle
  as the MissedCall platform usage costs.
- This is the lowest-$/hr, most time-intensive service in the portfolio per
  `PRICING.md` — reasonable to schedule last in the day/week after the
  higher-$/hr oversight work, consistent with the daily queue's ranking.
