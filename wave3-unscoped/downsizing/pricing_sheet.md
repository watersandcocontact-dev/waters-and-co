# Senior Downsizing — Pricing Sheet

**2026-08-06: split into two genuinely different services**, not just a
price tier — different scope, different liability/insurance profile. Both
live under the single `Downsizing` hub business line with a
`coordination`/`labour` task-type split (same pattern as GBP/MissedCall
setup vs management).

## Coordination (no physical labour)

Liaising with removalists, cleaners, charities, and real-estate agents —
scheduling, vendor comparison, timeline management. Vendor/removalist costs
pass through to the client, not bundled into the fee.

| Model | Price |
|---|---|
| Hourly | **$45–65/hr** |
| Packaged flat quote (full move coordination) | **$400–1,200** |

## Hands-on labour/sorting

Physical decluttering, sorting, and packing work.

| Model | Price |
|---|---|
| Hourly | **$80/hr flat** (set 2026-08-06, Owner's call — was a $50-80/hr range) |

## Competitor evidence (verified 2026-08-06)

**AU-specific downsizing-service pricing is genuinely unpublished** — Care
to Move (Brisbane), Home Moving Planners (Melbourne), We Move It (SEQ), and
Senior Moves (Melbourne) are all confirmed quote-only on their live sites.
This is the actual market norm for this exact service, not a research gap.

Comparison-point pricing (real, sourced):
- Removalists (2-person + truck): $100–200/hr nationally (airtasker.com/au/costs/removals)
- Professional organiser/declutter: $80–98/hr+GST; Airtasker task median $280, whole-home $500–1,200+ (airtasker.com/au/costs/decluttering, queensofclutter.com.au, organisemylife.com.au)
- Estate sale/liquidator commission: 30–50% of gross proceeds
- International senior-move-manager benchmark (context only, not AU-sourced): US$60–120/hr

## Reasoning

Coordination has near-zero delivery cost (phone/email/scheduling only), so
it can undercut $3,000–8,000+ bundled full-service operators by a wide
margin while charging near AU professional-organiser hourly rates for
comparable liaison work. Labour-only pricing is kept at the original
estimate since no AU competitor publishes a hands-on-only rate to check it
against.
