# Senior Downsizing/Cleanout — Service Scope & Process Checklist

Hands-on help for seniors/families downsizing, decluttering, or clearing a
property (e.g. before a move into aged care, or after a bereavement).
In-person by nature — local service, unlike most of the rest of this
portfolio.

## Process checklist

- [ ] **Initial walkthrough** — assess scope, rough timeline, any items
      needing special handling (valuables, sentimental items, documents)
- [ ] **Sort into categories** with the client/family present for decisions
      on anything ambiguous: keep, donate, sell, dispose, "decide later" box
      (don't force decisions on sentimental items in one sitting — this is
      often emotionally difficult, build in time)
- [ ] **Coordinate logistics**: removalist quotes if a move is involved,
      charity pickup (Salvos, Vinnies, Lifeline all do free pickups for
      donatable furniture/goods), council hard-rubbish or skip bin for
      disposal, secure document handling (shredding for anything with
      personal/financial info)
- [ ] **Day-of coordination**: be present or manage the removalists/charity
      pickup/disposal crew directly if the client wants a fully hands-off
      service
- [ ] **Final clean-through** — confirm nothing important was missed before
      final handover

## Important boundaries (don't cross these)

- **Don't handle or advise on anything that needs a professional**: high-value
  items (art, jewelry, antiques) should go to an actual valuer before
  disposal/sale decisions, not be assessed by you.
- **Don't make decisions on the client's behalf** for anything ambiguous or
  sentimental — always defer to them or their family, even if it slows
  things down. This is a trust-heavy, emotionally sensitive service.
- **Get things in writing** — a simple written agreement on what gets kept
  vs. disposed of before you dispose of anything, especially if working
  with an elderly client where family members might later dispute a
  decision that was made.
- If there's any indication of a formal deceased-estate process underway
  (executor duties, probate), that's a different, more legally complex
  situation — **hand off to the (currently unbuilt) Deceased-Estate Admin
  service scope or a solicitor**, don't blur the two.
