# AI Search Visibility Audit — Pricing Sheet

Flat-fee audit of whether a local business gets recommended by ChatGPT,
Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews, plus a prioritised 90-day fix
list. Best margin is the bolt-on to an existing GBP/ReviewGen client — no
cold-sale cost and the baseline is already known.

## Proposed pricing (drafted 2026-08-09 — NOT yet validated by delivery)

| Package | Price | Time | Effective $/hr |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free snapshot** — 3 prompts, 1 platform, headline finding only | **$0** | ~20min | lead magnet, not a service |
| **Standard audit** — full prompt set across 3 platforms, GBP + NAP + reviews + technical, written report and 90-day plan | **$249–349** | ~2.5–3hr | **$83–140/hr** |
| **Bolt-on for an existing GBP/ReviewGen client** | **$149–199** | ~1.5–2hr | **$75–133/hr** — best margin, baseline already known |
| **Re-test** — same prompt set re-run, before/after comparison | **$99–129** | ~1hr | $99–129/hr |
| Multi-location / franchise | quote only | — | out of scope for now, see service_scope.md |

**Suggested `RATE_CARD` entry if it becomes a line: $100/hr** (blended
midpoint across the standard audit and the bolt-on).

### Must be reconciled with the existing $99 GBP visibility check

The v2 handover pricing (uncommitted in the working tree as at 2026-08-09)
sets GBP's **"Visibility check and action plan" at $99 fixed** — a
diagnostic covering GBP, website journey, contact path, review signals and
local-search gaps, delivered as a ranked action plan. That overlaps most of
the standard audit above.

Treat the two as **one ladder, not two products**:

| Rung | What it adds | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Local visibility check (existing GBP offer) | GBP, website, reviews, local gaps → ranked plan | **$99** |
| + AI search visibility | live prompt testing across ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity, entity + NAP consistency, AI competitor comparison | **$249–349** |

Presented this way the increment is defensible — roughly $150–250 for the
platform testing and entity work, which is the genuinely new labour.
Presented as two separate products it is not, and it hands the "rebranded
SEO" objection a concrete example. **The $249–349 figures below were set
against external competitors before the $99 internal offer was known;** they
survive the comparison but the framing above should be adopted with them.

## Reasoning

- **$249–349 undercuts the direct Perth competitor ($497) without
  bottoming out.** It sits just above AI Local Link's $299 national offer
  at the top of the range and just below it at the bottom, which is the
  right place to be against a non-local competitor.
- **Not priced against the $27 self-serve tool** — that tool produces
  measurement, not interpretation or fixes, and competing with it on price
  would undervalue the report. It is, however, what a price-objecting
  prospect will bring up, so the answer needs to be ready: the tool tells
  you that you're invisible, it doesn't tell you why or what to do.
- **The bolt-on rate is where the real advantage is.** A competitor selling
  cold has customer-acquisition cost baked into $497. Selling to an
  existing GBP client removes that entirely, which is why $149–199 still
  clears $75–133/hr.
- **The free snapshot is table stakes, not generosity** — both direct AU
  competitors lead with one. Skipping it would be a competitive
  disadvantage; it also doubles as GBP/ReviewGen lead generation.

## Confidence and gaps

- **Competitor pricing: high confidence** — published figures fetched
  directly from the providers' own pages, not aggregator summaries.
- **Time estimates: low confidence** — the 2.5–3hr figure is an estimate,
  not measured. Nothing has been delivered. The effective $/hr column is
  therefore provisional and should be re-derived after the first 3–5 real
  audits, same as was done for Downsizing and Digital Legacy.
- **Unverified claim to watch:** an AU industry source asserts anything
  under $1,500 is rebranded SEO. Treat as a competitor's positioning claim
  rather than fact, but expect to meet it as a sales objection.
