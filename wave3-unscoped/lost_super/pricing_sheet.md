# Lost Super / TPD Navigation — Pricing Sheet (draft, adjustable)

Based on `PRICING.md` row 7 ($200-800/referred case, ~30-45min → $270-1,000+/hr,
"high but infrequent").

## Fee model: referral fee, not a client-facing price

Unlike every other service in this portfolio, **the client doesn't pay you
directly** — you're paid a referral fee by the specialist (TPD lawyer/AFSL
adviser) you refer a qualified lead to, once/if that lead results in a
claim. This means:

- **No client-facing pricing sheet needed** — there's nothing to quote a
  client, the lost-super search itself should be free/low-cost as a
  value-add (or bundled into another service you're already providing them,
  e.g. a Senior Tech Concierge session).
- **Negotiate the referral fee with your specialist partner(s) directly** —
  $200-800/referred case is the market-typical range per `PRICING.md`, but
  the actual number depends entirely on what you agree with the specific
  firm. Get this in writing before referring anyone.
- **Income is lumpy and infrequent** — a handful of qualified referrals a
  quarter, not a weekly revenue stream. Treat it as background/bonus income
  layered onto your other services (e.g. flagged during a Tech Concierge or
  GBP client conversation), not something to actively market standalone.

## Notes

- **Service area: Australia-wide** for the identification/navigation part
  (remote-friendly); your referral partner's own coverage may be narrower —
  confirm before referring someone outside their service area.
- Worth having 1-2 backup referral partners in case your primary one can't
  take a particular case (different states, different specialties).
