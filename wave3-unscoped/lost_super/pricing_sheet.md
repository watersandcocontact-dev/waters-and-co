# Lost Super & TPD/Insurance-in-Super Navigation — Pricing Sheet

## Model (confirmed 2026-08-06)

**Free to the client** for the lost-super search/navigation step (mirrors
the free ATO/myGov reality — Moneysmart explicitly warns "you don't need to
pay anyone to help you find your super... avoid third-party 'super finder'
services that charge fees"). Revenue comes entirely from a **referral fee**
paid by a no-win-no-fee TPD law firm when a client's case is referred and
accepted.

**The $200–800/referred-case figure in PRICING.md is plausible but
unverified** — no firm publishes referral-fee numbers publicly. This needs
a real partner negotiation, not more research, to pin down.

## Regulatory checks completed (2026-08-06)

1. ATO "Find lost super" via myGov confirmed still free.
2. **Contingency/percentage fees on compensation are illegal for lawyers in
   Australia** — any referral fee must be flat, not a % of the client's
   TPD payout. This shapes the $200-800 flat-fee model as the right shape,
   even with the number unconfirmed.
3. Referral fees to non-lawyer referrers are legal, provided the client is
   disclosed the arrangement.
4. **Open item:** claims-farming bans exist for CTP motor-accident and
   workers-comp claims specifically (e.g. Qld, fines to ~$48,390) — not
   confirmed whether any state/federal ban extends to TPD/super claims.
   Keep the model strictly client-initiated (never cold-called) regardless,
   to stay clearly on the right side of this either way.
