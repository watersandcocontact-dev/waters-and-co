## Recurring topic bank (rotate through these)

1. **Before/after job photo** — the single best-performing post type for trades.
2. **Seasonal offer/reminder** — e.g. pre-winter heater checks, storm season gutter clears.
3. **Team/behind-the-scenes** — humanizes the business, low effort.
4. **Customer review highlight** — screenshot or quote a recent 5-star review (get permission if using their name/photo).
5. **Service spotlight** — deep-dive one service per month (e.g. "Did you know we also do X?").
6. **FAQ answer** — turn a common customer question into a post.
7. **Local/community tie-in** — sponsorships, local job sites, community events.
8. **Emergency/availability reminder** — "still taking same-day callouts this week."

## Cadence guide

- Minimum viable: 1 post/week + photo upload.
- Month 1 for new clients: 2 posts/week to show fast visible improvement (ties to the "quick win" pitch).
- Photos: aim for 4-8 new photos/month minimum, ideally client-supplied from real jobs (ask them to text photos after each job).
