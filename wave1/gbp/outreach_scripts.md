# GBP Outreach — 3 Message Variants

Personalize the [bracketed] hooks using the specific weak point found in the audit
(see `audit_checklist.md` / `audit_tool.py` output) before every send/call.

**Service area: Australia-wide.** The call and text/email scripts work for any
prospect anywhere in the country — nothing about GBP cleanup or management
requires being physically near the client. The in-person script (#2) is just
one optional channel for prospects you happen to be near; don't read it as
implying the business only serves local trades. Lead with phone/text/email for
national reach, and treat in-person as a bonus for local opportunities.

---

## 1. Cold call script

**Opening (10-15 sec):**
"Hi, is this [Owner name]? Hey [Name], I'm [Your name] — I help local trades show
up better on Google when people search for [trade] near them. I actually looked
up [Business name] on Google Maps just now — quick one: are you the person who
manages your Google listing, or is that someone else?"

*(If not them — ask who is, get a warm intro or number.)*

**Hook (the audit finding):**
"Cool — so the reason I called is I noticed [specific finding, e.g. 'you haven't
posted anything on your Google profile in over 6 months, and you've only got 8
reviews showing' / 'your photos are pretty outdated']. That stuff directly affects
whether you show up in the map results when someone searches '[trade] near me' —
it's one of the biggest ranking factors Google uses locally."

**Value/offer:**
"What I do is a one-off cleanup — fix the profile, get fresh photos and posts up,
tighten up categories and hours — plus I can keep it actively managed month to
month so it doesn't go stale again. Would it be worth me sending you a quick
before/after style breakdown of what I found, no charge, so you can see exactly
what's costing you visibility?"

**Close:**
"Great — what's the best email or number to send that to? I'll get it over today/
this week."

---

## 2. In-person pitch (on-site / trade counter / job site)

"Hey mate, quick one — do you handle your business's Google listing, or is that
someone else? [pause] I do local SEO for trades — basically making sure you show
up when people search '[trade] near [suburb]' on Google. I had a look at yours
before I came by and [specific finding — e.g. 'noticed you're not showing up with
many recent photos or posts, and your review count's lower than a couple of your
competitors nearby']. That's usually a quick fix. I'm doing free audits for a
few local trades this month — want me to send you what I found? Takes 2 minutes,
no obligation, and you'll see exactly what's holding your listing back."

*(Hand over a card / get their number or email on the spot.)*

---

## 3. Text / email outreach

**Subject (email):** Quick find on [Business name]'s Google listing

**Body:**

> Hi [Name],
>
> I help local trades in [suburb/area] show up better on Google Maps when people
> search for a [trade] nearby. I had a quick look at [Business name]'s Google
> Business Profile and noticed a few things likely costing you visibility:
>
> - [Finding 1 — e.g. no posts in the last 6+ months]
> - [Finding 2 — e.g. only 8 reviews vs 30+ for nearby competitors]
> - [Finding 3 — e.g. photos look several years old]
>
> These are some of the biggest local-ranking factors Google uses, so fixing them
> usually shows up fast in map-pack visibility. I do a one-off profile cleanup
> ($150-300 depending on scope) plus ongoing monthly management if you want it
> kept fresh ($100-200/month).
>
> Happy to send a quick before/after style breakdown for free, no obligation —
> want me to put one together?
>
> [Your name]
> [Phone] · [Email]

**SMS (short version):**
"Hi [Name], [Your name] here — I help local trades show up better on Google
Maps. Had a look at [Business]'s listing, noticed a couple of quick wins (low
review count, no recent posts). Want me to send a free 2-min breakdown of what
I found? No obligation."
