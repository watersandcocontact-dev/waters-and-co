# GBP Cold-Outreach Targets — Perth, WA Trade Businesses

**Purpose:** Desk research to identify real trade businesses in Perth, Western
Australia with visibly thin or outdated Google Business Profiles (GBP), for
cold-outreach prospecting by a solo GBP/local-SEO optimization service.

**Important notes before using this list:**

- This is desk research based on publicly viewable Google Maps / Google
  Search listings, checked on **29 July 2026**. Google profiles change
  constantly — **re-verify each listing immediately before outreach** (review
  count, hours, photos, and especially whether they've since added a website
  or Posts).
- **Perth was confirmed as the operator's actual home city/target market**
  (the operator is a solo entrepreneur based in Perth, WA). This replaces an
  earlier Melbourne-based list (`wave1/gbp/target_businesses.md`) that was
  produced before the operator's city was confirmed — that file is kept only
  as background/reference and is not the operationally relevant list.
- All listings below were viewed via Google's public, non-signed-in Maps and
  Search interface. That view does **not** reliably surface the "Google Posts
  / Updates" feature for most listings — for most businesses on this list,
  **Posts activity could not be directly confirmed one way or the other** and
  is called out as "not confirmed" rather than guessed. Two businesses on
  this list (Ace Termite Treatment Kalamunda, Shield Locksmiths Midland WA)
  happened to have a visible "From the owner" post/description with a date
  stamp, which is noted explicitly where relevant — in Shield Locksmiths'
  case the post is well over 30 days old (stale); in Ace Termite's case it is
  only marginally over 30 days old, which is flagged transparently rather
  than overstated.
- Where no street address is shown on a listing (common for trade
  businesses that operate as service-area businesses without a public
  storefront), the business's general service area/suburb is noted instead
  and this is called out in the table.
- No personal information about business owners is included. This is
  standard public-listing competitive/prospecting research, kept factual —
  no commentary on the owners themselves.

## How I found these

1. Used Google's local-pack search results (`google.com/search?q=<trade>+<suburb>+wa&tbm=lcl`)
   for a spread of trade + suburb combinations across the Perth metro area —
   inner (Fremantle, Bayswater/Victoria Park area), northern (Joondalup,
   Wanneroo), southern (Rockingham, Kwinana/Mandogalup, Gosnells/Huntingdale),
   eastern (Midland, Kalamunda), and south-eastern (Armadale) — for plumber,
   electrician, landscaper, builder, roofer, painter, tiler, handyman, pest
   control, and locksmith. This view returns the full local-pack list
   (typically 15-20 real listings per query) with star rating and review
   count visible for each, which made it possible to scan for low-review-count
   or poor-rating businesses efficiently.
2. Flagged candidates with review counts under roughly 15, sub-4.0 ratings, or
   a "No reviews" tag, prioritizing ones with an identifiable Perth-metro
   suburb attached to the listing.
3. Opened each flagged business's own Google Maps profile page directly (via
   `google.com/maps/search/<business>+<suburb>+WA/` and following the result
   link to the full place page) to verify, first-hand: exact review count and
   review dates, star rating, presence/absence of a street address, presence/
   absence of a website, presence/absence of an hours configuration ("Add
   hours" prompt = not configured), presence/absence of a business
   description ("From the owner" section), and which photo categories (if
   any) existed.
4. Excluded larger/heavily-reviewed competitors (hundreds of reviews, clearly
   well-maintained profiles) and excluded a few "No reviews" listings that
   looked like likely duplicate/orphaned entries for a business that already
   had a stronger primary listing, since the goal was plausible, currently-
   operating small/independent trade businesses worth a genuine outreach
   conversation.
5. Did not fabricate or estimate any figures. Where a detail (e.g. Posts
   activity for most listings) could not be confirmed through the available
   public view, that limitation is stated explicitly in the table or the
   notes above rather than guessed at.
