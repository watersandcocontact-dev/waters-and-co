# Google Business Profile (GBP) Audit Checklist

Use this on every prospect before outreach, and on every client monthly. Score each
item, sum for a /100 total. Anything under 60 is a strong outreach target; anything
under 40 is a "quick win" pitch (dramatic before/after possible fast).

Pair with `audit_tool.py` in this folder to turn checklist answers into a score
automatically — or just tally by hand.

## Scoring rubric (100 pts total)

| # | Item | What to check | Points |
|---|------|----------------|--------|
| 1 | Photos present & recent | ≥10 photos, at least 1 added in last 90 days | 15 |
| 2 | Google Posts activity | A Post published in the last 30 days | 15 |
| 3 | Review count | ≥15 reviews = full marks; scale down below that | 15 |
| 4 | Review response rate | % of reviews with an owner reply, especially recent ones | 15 |
| 5 | Review response speed | Replies posted within ~1 week of the review | 5 |
| 6 | Hours accuracy | Hours match what's on their website/socials, incl. public holidays | 10 |
| 7 | Category correctness | Primary category matches the actual trade; relevant secondary categories set | 10 |
| 8 | Business description | Filled in, keyword-relevant, not generic boilerplate | 5 |
| 9 | Website/booking link present | Link works and goes to a live, relevant page | 5 |
| 10 | NAP consistency | Name/Address/Phone matches website & other directories | 5 |

## Detailed checks

**1. Photos**
- Count total photos. Under 10 = weak.
- Check date of most recent photo (Google shows "X months ago" on hover/click).
- Look for: exterior/van/uniform shots, before/after job photos, team photos. Stock-only or zero photos = strong red flag (and strong pitch material).

**2. Posts**
- Google Posts appear on the profile under "Updates". No posts, or last post >30 days old = weak.
- Note: many trades never use Posts at all — this is usually the single easiest "wow" fix to demonstrate value fast.

**3. Review count**
- Under 15 total reviews for an established business = under-asking for reviews.
- Compare to 2-3 direct local competitors — if they have 3-5x more, that's a competitive gap to pitch.

**4-5. Review responses**
- Click through the most recent 10-15 reviews. Count how many have an owner response.
- Note response time where visible (Google shows reply date vs review date).
- Zero responses, or only responses to 5-star reviews (ignoring 1-3 star) = weak reputation management, good ReviewGen service pitch too.

**6. Hours**
- Compare listed hours against the business's own website/Facebook.
- Check public holiday hours if the business has posted about them — GBP shows a warning if hours look outdated after major holidays.

**7. Category**
- Wrong or overly generic primary category (e.g. "Contractor" instead of "Electrician") hurts local pack ranking directly.
- Missing relevant secondary categories = missed searches.

**8. Description**
- Look for keyword stuffing (bad) vs a generic 1-liner (also bad) vs a clear, locally-relevant description mentioning suburb/service area and specific services (good).

**9. Website link**
- Click it. Dead link, parked domain, or a generic homepage instead of a relevant landing page all count as weak.

**10. NAP consistency**
- Spot-check name/address/phone against their Facebook page and website footer. Mismatches hurt local SEO and confuse customers.

## After the audit

For every prospect, write 1-2 sentences of the single biggest, fastest fix (e.g. "0 posts in the last 6 months, 40 photos never updated since 2019 — quick win: 4 posts + 10 new photos in week 1") — this becomes the hook line in outreach.
