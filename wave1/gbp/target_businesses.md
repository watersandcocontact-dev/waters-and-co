# GBP Cold-Outreach Targets — Melbourne Trade Businesses

**Purpose:** Desk research to identify real trade businesses in Melbourne, VIC with
visibly thin or outdated Google Business Profiles (GBP), for cold-outreach
prospecting by a solo GBP/local-SEO optimization service.

**Important notes before using this list:**

- This is desk research based on publicly viewable Google Maps / Google Search
  listings, checked on **29 July 2026**. Google profiles change constantly —
  **re-verify each listing immediately before outreach** (review count, hours,
  photos, and especially whether they've since added a website or posts).
- **Melbourne was used as a default city** because the operator's actual home
  city/target market wasn't specified. If the operator is based elsewhere or
  wants a different metro area, this list should be redone for that location.
- All listings below were viewed via Google's public, non-signed-in Maps
  interface. That view does **not** reliably surface the "Google Posts /
  Updates" feature, so **Posts activity could not be confirmed one way or the
  other for any business on this list** — treat "no recent Posts" as a
  reasonable inference (very few small trade businesses use Posts at all,
  and none of these had any indication of a Posts tab), not a hard-confirmed
  fact. Everything else in the "weak profile signals" column was directly
  observed on the business's own Google Maps listing.
- No personal information about business owners is included. This is standard
  public-listing competitive/prospecting research, kept factual — no
  commentary on the owners themselves.

## How I found these

1. Searched Google Maps (public, non-signed-in view) by trade + suburb, using a
   deliberate spread across Melbourne: inner north (Brunswick, Preston), west
   (Sunshine West), inner/outer east (Croydon, Mont Albert/Box Hill), south-east
   (Frankston, Cranbourne West, Dandenong), north (Reservoir), and outer west
   (Melton) — covering electrician, landscaper, builder/renovator, roofer,
   painter, tiler, handyman, locksmith, plumber, and pest control.
2. For each trade/suburb search, scanned the returned list of real listings and
   flagged any business with a low review count (roughly under 15), a visibly
   generic or missing description, missing website/phone, or reviews that were
   old/inactive or unanswered.
3. Opened each flagged business's own Google Maps profile page directly to
   confirm the details in the table above first-hand — review count and text,
   photo count, presence/absence of a website, description, hours, and category
   — rather than relying on the summary card from the results list.
4. Excluded larger/heavily-reviewed competitors (e.g. franchise-style operators
   with hundreds of reviews) and one locksmith listing with an address pattern
   that looked inconsistent with a genuine local business, since the goal was
   plausible small/independent operators worth a genuine outreach conversation.
5. Did not fabricate or estimate any figures — where a detail (e.g. Posts
   activity) couldn't be confirmed through the available view, that limitation
   is called out above rather than guessed.
