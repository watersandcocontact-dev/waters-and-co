# WA — Land Valuation Objection Process

**Researched:** 2026-07-29
**Caveat:** Deadlines, forms, portal URLs, and processing timeframes below were verified against official .gov.au sources (landgate.wa.gov.au, wa.gov.au) on the research date, but state government processes and forms change periodically. Re-verify all details directly against the official sources below before advising any client.

---

## Authority

- **Landgate** (Western Australian Land Information Authority) — through the **Valuer-General**, issues property valuations (Gross Rental Value/GRV and Unimproved Value/UV) under the *Valuation of Land Act 1978* (WA) for local government rates, water rates (via Water Corporation), land tax, and pastoral rent purposes.
- **RevenueWA** (WA Department of Treasury and Finance) — administers and issues the **land tax assessment** itself (applying Landgate's valuation to calculate the tax) — a separate function from valuing the land. As in other states, the land value objection goes to the valuing authority (Landgate/Valuer-General), not to RevenueWA.
- Local governments and Water Corporation issue rates notices carrying the Landgate-determined value but do not themselves determine or handle objections to the value.

## Deadline

- **60 days** from the date of issue stated on the relevant notice — local government rates notice, water rates notice, land tax assessment notice, or pastoral rent notice.
- Extensions beyond 60 days require Valuer-General approval and are assessed on "reasonable cause" grounds — not guaranteed.

## How to Object

Three lodgement methods:
1. **Online:** Landgate's online objection lodgement / "Valuation Services Client Portal & Application for Objection Portal" — allows account creation, document upload, and saving drafts. See: https://www.landgate.wa.gov.au/valuations/valuations-for-rates-and-taxes/new-valuation-system/client-and-objection-submission-portal-terms-and-conditions/ (portal terms/access page — confirm the direct application URL when lodging, as the exact login link was not independently confirmed in this research).
2. **Email:** vs@landgate.wa.gov.au, attaching the completed **Objection to Valuation** form.
3. **Post / in person:** The Valuer-General, Landgate, PO Box 2222, Midland WA 6936 (or hand-deliver to a Landgate Customer Service desk — note Perth and Bunbury offices are both referenced in Landgate/secondary materials for the Property & Valuations Branch).

The generic **Objection to Valuation** form (also distributed via some local council websites, e.g. Shire of Woodanilling) is the standard form used regardless of channel for a paper lodgement.

## Evidence / Grounds Accepted

- **GRV (Gross Rental Value) objections** — typically for residential/commercial rated properties: rental evidence on comparable properties, or other pertinent information, at or around the relevant date of valuation.
- **UV (Unimproved Value) objections** — typically for rural/land-tax-relevant land: vacant land sales evidence on comparable land, or other pertinent information, at or around the date of valuation.
- **Pastoral rent objections:** require direct contact with Landgate's pastoral valuers rather than the standard process.

## What Happens After Lodging

- Landgate notifies the applicant within **10 working days** if additional information is required to progress the objection.
- The objection is allocated to a valuer, who reviews the evidence and analyses it against market values as at the date of valuation.
- Due to complexity, some objections can take **up to 12 months** to finalise.
- The applicant is informed by letter of the Valuer-General's final decision: **disallowed** (original value stands) or **allowed** (value adjusted).
- Rates/land tax must still be paid by the due date regardless of a pending objection — lodging an objection does not defer the liability, and penalty tax can apply for late payment of the original assessment.

## Escalation Path

- **State Administrative Tribunal (SAT)** — a dissatisfied applicant may request referral to SAT within **60 days of receiving the Valuer-General's written decision**.
