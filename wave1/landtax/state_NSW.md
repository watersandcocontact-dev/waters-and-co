# NSW — Land Valuation Objection Process

**Researched:** 2026-07-29
**Caveat:** Deadlines, forms, portal URLs, and processing timeframes below were verified against official .gov.au / nsw.gov.au sources on the research date, but state government processes and forms change periodically. Re-verify all details (especially current deadlines, form versions, and fees) directly against the official sources below before advising any client.

---

## Authority

- **Valuer General of NSW** — the independent statutory officer responsible for determining land values under the *Valuation of Land Act 1916* (NSW).
- **Value NSW** — the operational unit within the Department of Planning, Housing and Infrastructure that manages the valuation system and objections process on behalf of the Valuer General.
- **Revenue NSW** — administers land tax *assessments* (applying the land value to calculate tax) — a distinct process from objecting to the land value itself. Council rates use the same Valuer General land values.
- Note: there are effectively **two objection pathways** in NSW:
  1. Object to the **land value** itself → Valuer General / Value NSW.
  2. Object to the **land tax assessment** (e.g. incorrect application of the law, exemptions, thresholds) → Revenue NSW.

## Deadline

- **60 days** to lodge a land value objection, measured from either:
  - the objection closing date printed on the Notice of Valuation, or
  - 60 days from the issue date on the land tax assessment notice (if objecting via a land tax notice).
- Late objections are rarely accepted — a written explanation for the delay must be provided and is at the Valuer General's discretion.
- Land tax **assessment** objections (with Revenue NSW) must also be lodged within 60 days of the issue date on the assessment notice.

## How to Object

- **Primary channel:** NSW Valuation and Objection Portal — https://valuation.property.nsw.gov.au/ — requires creating an account (landowner, joint owner, strata owner, lessee, or authorised managing agent/representative). Login is secured with multi-factor authentication.
- All supporting evidence should be uploaded **at the time of lodging** — the portal process is generally intended to be a single, complete submission (limited ability to add evidence afterward).
- **Non-online option:** call 1800 110 038 to request an objection information kit (for those who don't wish to lodge online).
- A **separate objection must be lodged for each land value / property** if objecting to more than one.
- If land is jointly owned or leased, other landholders should be notified that an objection is being lodged.
- Revenue NSW land tax assessment objections: lodge online via the official form, or email objection@revenue.nsw.gov.au, including Client ID, contact details, and supporting documents.

## Evidence / Grounds Accepted

- Land value is incorrectly assessed (valuation is too high/low).
- Factual errors in the Notice of Valuation or assessment (e.g. incorrect area, dimensions, property description, or apportionment of value between interests).
- **Evidence required:** Value NSW guidance indicates objectors should provide **at least 3 comparable sales** supporting the contended land value, with addresses, sale dates, and an explanation of comparability. Objections without supporting evidence risk being disallowed.
- For land tax **assessment** objections (Revenue NSW): grounds are that the law was incorrectly applied to the assessment (e.g. exemption eligibility, threshold, ownership details) — not the land value itself.

## What Happens After Lodging

- A different valuer than the original is assigned to review the objection, examining the evidence and comparable sales.
- Objector may receive a preliminary/draft report and reportedly has around **14 days** to flag any factual errors in it.
- The Valuer General aims to notify the outcome **within 90 days** of lodging (Revenue NSW also cites up to 90 days processing for assessment objections).
- Land tax/rates must still be paid by the due date while the objection is pending — interest accrues on overdue amounts if not paid; a refund (with interest) is issued if the objection succeeds.

## Escalation Path

- **Land value objections:** Appeal to the **Land and Environment Court of NSW** within 60 days of the date the objection determination letter is issued.
  - Contact: (02) 9113 8200; www.lec.justice.nsw.gov.au
- **Land tax assessment objections (Revenue NSW):** External review via the **NSW Civil and Administrative Tribunal (NCAT)** or the **NSW Supreme Court** after a formal objection decision.
