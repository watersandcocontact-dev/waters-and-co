# Review Request Templates — Australian Consumer Law (ACL) Compliant

**Service area: Australia-wide.** Setting up and managing a client's review-
request flow and AI response drafts is entirely remote (SMS/email tool
configuration + ongoing monitoring) — no in-person requirement, so market and
onboard clients from anywhere in the country.

## Compliance rules baked into these templates (read before customizing)

Under the Australian Consumer Law and ACCC guidance on online reviews:

- **Ask every customer, not just happy ones.** Do NOT filter/gate — i.e. never ask
  "how was your experience?" first and only send the review link to people who
  answer positively while diverting unhappy customers elsewhere. That's considered
  a misleading review practice. Send the same review ask to everyone.
- **No incentives for leaving a review** (no discounts, prize draws, freebies) —
  incentivized reviews must be disclosed if ever used, and disclosure requirements
  are strict enough that the simplest compliant approach is: no incentive, ever.
- **Never write, edit, or post a review on the customer's behalf**, and never ask
  them to say specific things — you can prompt for what to mention (see below) but
  the words must be the customer's own.
  **Never offer to write a good review for them in exchange for anything.**
  **Never create fake accounts or ask friends/family to post as if they were customers.**
- Keep requests genuine, timely (send soon after job completion while it's fresh),
  and low-pressure (one initial ask + max one polite follow-up).

## Template 1 — SMS (send to every completed-job customer, ~2-24h after job)

> Hi [First name], it's [Business name] — thanks for having us out today! If you
> have 30 seconds, we'd really appreciate an honest review on Google, good or bad,
> it helps us a lot: [Google review link]
> Thanks again — [Tradesperson name]

## Template 2 — Email (send to every completed-job customer)

**Subject:** How did we go, [First name]?

> Hi [First name],
>
> Thanks for choosing [Business name] for your [service type] on [date]. We'd
> genuinely appreciate your honest feedback — it helps other locals find us and
> helps us know what we're doing well (or not).
>
> [Leave a Google review — button/link]
>
> If anything wasn't right, please just reply to this email directly and we'll
> sort it out.
>
> Thanks again,
> [Business name]

## Template 3 — Follow-up (only if no response after ~5-7 days, one follow-up max)

> Hi [First name], just a friendly follow-up — if you have a moment, we'd really
> appreciate your honest review of the [service type] we did on [date]:
> [Google review link]. No worries at all if you'd rather not — thanks either way!

## Prompt for what to mention (optional, keeps it genuine — do not script exact words)

When asking, you can suggest a general focus without dictating content, e.g.:
"Feel free to mention whatever stood out — response time, the quality of the
work, communication, tidiness, whatever was true for you."

## What NOT to do (compliance guardrails)

- Don't only text/email customers you think were happy.
- Don't offer a discount on their invoice, a gift card, or entry into a draw for
  leaving a review.
- Don't ask them to mention specific keywords "for SEO" — that reads as coached/
  fake and both Google and the ACCC take issue with it.
- Don't post reviews yourself or ask staff/family to post as "customers."
