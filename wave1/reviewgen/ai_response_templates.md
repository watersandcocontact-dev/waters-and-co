# AI Review-Response Templates

These are starting drafts for an AI (or the business owner) to personalize per
review — always fill in the [bracketed] specifics before posting; never post a
template verbatim. Keep responses genuine, specific, and brief (Google surfaces
short, sincere replies best).

## 1. Five-star, with detail — Thank you

> Thanks so much for the kind words, [First name]! Really glad we could sort out
> your [specific issue mentioned, e.g. "hot water system"] and that [specific
> detail they praised, e.g. "the team turned up on time"]. We appreciate you
> taking the time to leave a review — see you next time!
> — [Business name]

## 2. Five-star, no detail (just stars/short text) — Thank you

> Thanks for the 5 stars, [First name] — really appreciate it! Let us know if
> you ever need anything else.
> — [Business name]

## 3. Three-star, mixed/neutral — Address the concern, invite dialogue

> Thanks for the honest feedback, [First name]. Glad [thing that went well] worked
> out, but sorry to hear about [specific concern raised] — that's not the standard
> we aim for. We'd like to make it right — could you give us a call on [phone] or
> reply here so we can sort it out?
> — [Business name]

## 4. One-star — Recovery, take it offline, no defensiveness

> Hi [First name], sorry to hear about your experience — this isn't what we want
> for any customer. We'd really like to understand what happened and put it
> right. Could you please call/email us directly at [phone/email] so we can look
> into it properly? Thank you for letting us know.
> — [Business name]

*(Never argue publicly, never dispute facts in the public reply — move it to a
private channel. If the review is factually false/abusive, that's a separate
"flag for removal via Google" process, not a public argument.)*

## 5. One-star, no detail given ("bad service" with nothing specific) — Ask for context, stay professional

> Hi [First name], we're sorry to hear you weren't happy — we'd genuinely like to
> understand what went wrong so we can fix it. Could you give us a call on
> [phone] or send us more detail? Thank you.
> — [Business name]

## Tone rules for the AI drafting these

- Always use the reviewer's first name if available.
- Reference something specific from their review — never fully generic.
- Never get defensive or argue publicly, even if the review seems unfair.
- Keep it under ~60 words.
- Sign off with the business name.
- For 1-2 star reviews: always invite them to a private channel (phone/email),
  never resolve the substance of a complaint in the public reply.
- Flag reviews with slurs, clear falsehoods about facts (e.g. wrong business),
  or spam for human review before any AI auto-post — don't auto-respond to those.
