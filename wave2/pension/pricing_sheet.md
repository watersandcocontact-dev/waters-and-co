# Age Pension / Centrelink Claim & Appeal Assistance — Pricing Sheet

Paperwork/process navigation only — **not financial/investment advice**.

## Pricing

| Service | Price |
|---|---|
| Age Pension claim lodgement assistance | **$349 flat**, disclosed up front |
| ARO (Authorised Review Officer) appeal/review assistance | **$249 flat** |

No ineligibility penalty clause — unlike the main incumbent (see below).

## Reasoning

$349 undercuts My Age Pension's cheapest all-in package ($660) by nearly
half and beats Claim My Pension's "from $550" — a flat, disclosed-upfront,
no-ineligibility-penalty structure directly answers the two least-transparent
incumbents' pricing opacity (PensionLink and Age Pension Solutions both
publish zero pricing on their own sites).
