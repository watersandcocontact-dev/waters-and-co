# Age Pension Claim — Document Checklist

**Research date:** 29 July 2026
**Sources:** servicesaustralia.gov.au (official Services Australia / Centrelink pages and forms), cross-checked with secondary summaries. See Sources list at the end. This is a preparation aid only — **not financial advice**, and not a substitute for the client's own claim on their myGov/Centrelink account.

> ⚠️ **Re-verify before use with clients.** Services Australia updates claim forms, document lists and thresholds regularly (asset/income test limits change 1 January, 1 July and 20 March/September each year; forms are revised periodically — note the SA002 form on file was version 2305 (May 2023) and the CI006/AUS140 notes were 2024 revisions at the time of this research). ## 1. Before starting: eligibility and timing basics

- Age Pension age is currently **67** (reached its final scheduled increase from 1 July 2023). Confirm current age on the Services Australia site as policy could change.
- A claim can be lodged **up to 13 weeks before** the client reaches Age Pension age (a separate, unrelated use of "13 weeks" from the review/appeal time limit discussed in the ARO template — do not conflate the two). Claiming earlier than the window is not possible; claiming very late does not get backdated to the eligibility date, so timing matters.
- If the client already receives another eligible Centrelink payment, Services Australia may send an **invitation to transfer to Age Pension** roughly 13 weeks out — this can be actioned online, or by paper form if the client has no online account.
- Claims can be made **online via myGov** (linked to a Centrelink account) or by **paper claim** (Claim for Age Pension and Pension Bonus, form **SA002**), or in some cases by phone/in person at a service centre.
- After lodging, Centrelink will generate a **specific list of supporting documents** it wants for that claim (this varies by individual circumstances) — the checklist below is a comprehensive superset to help a client gather likely-needed items in advance, not a guarantee of the exact list Centrelink will request.
- **Time limit to supply requested documents: 14 days** from the date requested, or the claim may be rejected/refused. If a client needs more time, they should contact Centrelink before the deadline to ask for an extension.

## 2. Identity documents

Centrelink requires proof of identity using a **points-based / category system** (typically evidence from multiple categories, e.g. one primary "with photo" document plus others). Typical acceptable documents include:

- [ ] Passport (Australian or foreign, current or expired within a set period)
- [ ] Australian driver's licence or Photo Card
- [ ] Birth certificate or extract
- [ ] Medicare card
- [ ] Citizenship certificate (if applicable)
- [ ] Visa/immigration documents (if not an Australian citizen — e.g. permanent residency evidence)
- [ ] Marriage certificate or change-of-name documents (if name differs across documents)
- [ ] Current address proof (e.g. rates notice, utility bill, lease) if requested

If claiming as a couple, **identity documents are required for both members of the couple**, even if only one is claiming.

---

## 3. Residence and citizenship

- [ ] Evidence of Australian citizenship or permanent residency
- [ ] Evidence of date of arrival in Australia (if not born in Australia) — passport stamps, immigration paperwork
- [ ] Details of countries lived in and time spent overseas (relevant to Australian Working Life Residence and possible international social security agreements)
- [ ] Details of any overseas pension entitlement (see Income section)

---

## 4. Relationship / household details

- [ ] Marriage certificate, or evidence of de facto relationship (if partnered)
- [ ] Separation/divorce documents (if relevant to relationship status)
- [ ] Partner's income and asset details (a couple's combined position is assessed even if only one partner is eligible/claiming)

---

## 5. Bank accounts and cash assets

- [ ] Statements for **all** bank, credit union and building society accounts (savings, cheque, term deposits, offset accounts) — for the client and, if partnered, the partner — usually the most recent statement showing balance and account details
- [ ] Details of any cash holdings above minor amounts

---

## 6. Income details

- [ ] Payslips (if still working) — recent payslips or an employer income statement
- [ ] Details of self-employment/business income (see Business section)
- [ ] Details of any Australian income support/other Centrelink or DVA payments currently received
- [ ] Details of **overseas pensions, annuities or income streams** — including foreign pension reference/claim numbers, as these can affect entitlement and may require the client to also claim any comparable foreign pension
- [ ] Managed investment / income stream statements

---

## 7. Assets — real estate

- [ ] Proof of home ownership and status as principal home (e.g. rates notice) — the principal home is normally exempt from the assets test but still needs to be declared
- [ ] Details/value of **any other real estate** owned (investment properties, holiday homes, vacant land) — market valuation evidence may be requested
- [ ] Details of outstanding mortgages or loans secured against any property
- [ ] Details of any granny flat interest / life interest arrangements

---

## 8. Assets — superannuation and investments

- [ ] Superannuation statements — both **accumulation phase** and **income stream (pension) phase** accounts, for client and partner
- [ ] Managed funds, shares, and other investment statements
- [ ] Details of any **income streams** (account-based pensions, annuities) including start date, purchase price, and current balance
- [ ] Details of **funeral bonds or prepaid funeral plans** (must be declared even though typically exempt from the assets test up to a threshold)
- [ ] Life insurance policies with a surrender value

---

## 9. Assets — business, trusts, companies

- [ ] Business financial statements (profit & loss, balance sheet) if self-employed
- [ ] Details of any interest in a **private trust or private company**, including trust deeds, distributions, and structure
- [ ] Farm or primary production asset details, if applicable

---

## 10. Gifting and deprivation

- [ ] Details of any assets or money **gifted or disposed of** in the last 5 years (gifting rules can count disposed assets toward the assets test for up to 5 years)

---

## 11. Other forms that may be required

Depending on circumstances, Centrelink may require additional forms alongside the core claim, for example:

- [ ] **CI006** — Information you need to know about your claim for Age Pension and Pension Bonus
- [ ] Income and Assets form (if not fully captured in the online claim)
- [ ] Real Estate details form
- [ ] Managed Investments form
- [ ] Details of Income Stream form
- [ ] Overseas pension claim/enquiry documents (e.g. AUS140-series "Notes for claiming Australian Pension" used for international agreement claims)
- [ ] Rent Assistance evidence (private rental — lease and rent receipts), if applicable
- [ ] Bank account details (BSB/account number) for payment purposes

Check the ["Other forms you may need to complete"](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/individuals/services/centrelink/age-pension/how-claim/other-forms-you-may-need-complete) page for the current, circumstance-specific list.

## 12. Practical prep checklist for the entrepreneur's client engagement

- [ ] Confirm client's Age Pension age and earliest lodgement date (13-week window)
- [ ] Confirm whether client has an active myGov account linked to Centrelink; if not, discuss setting one up (client must do this themselves / with their own credentials — see note below)
- [ ] Gather identity documents for client (+ partner if applicable)
- [ ] Gather 12 months (or as requested) of bank statements for all accounts
- [ ] Gather superannuation statements (accumulation + pension phase)
- [ ] Gather real estate ownership/valuation evidence
- [ ] Gather details of any trusts, companies, or business interests
- [ ] Gather details of income streams, annuities, overseas pensions
- [ ] Note any gifting in the last 5 years
- [ ] Prepare a summary income & assets worksheet to help the client complete the online claim accurately
- [ ] Remind client that supporting documents must be provided within **14 days** of Centrelink's request

> **Important — scope boundary:** This checklist is a preparation/navigation aid. The entrepreneur should help the client assemble and organise these documents, but the client must lodge the claim themselves through their own myGov-authenticated Centrelink online account (or paper/in-person channel), since the entrepreneur does not have — and should not seek — the client's myGov login credentials. This is not financial or legal advice.
