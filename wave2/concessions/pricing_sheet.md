# Energy & Concession/Rebate Navigation — Pricing Sheet

## Verdict: NOT a viable standalone paid line

Two independent search passes (2026-08-06) found **zero AU businesses
charging specifically for energy/utility concession-rebate application
assistance**. This is covered by well-funded free channels:

- Service NSW / state-equivalent portals — free online/in-person application
- National Debt Helpline / free financial counsellors (1800 007 007)
- Victoria's Energy Assistance Program — free, government-funded
- National Seniors Australia's free Concessions Calculator

**Important correction to the original premise:** several state rebates
(e.g. QLD electricity) now **auto-renew** once a concession card is
registered on the retailer account — it's a one-time registration, not an
annual re-application, for those rebates. This weakens the recurring pain
point the line was originally scoped around.

The only genuine paid comparable found — Claim My Pension (Centrelink
claims/reviews, $330–550+) — prices at that level only because the
underlying benefit (Age Pension) is large and the process genuinely complex.
A $200–400/yr energy rebate doesn't clear that bar; a $550 fee to chase a
$200 rebate is an obvious non-starter, and even a percentage model (cf. Bill
Experts' 30%-of-savings bill-negotiation service) would only justify
$60–120, which still reads as predatory against free help.

## Recommendation

**Fold into Tech Concierge / Downsizing visits as a free value-add**, not a
marketed standalone service. If ever charged in isolation: a token **$0–25
add-on** for the extra 15-20 minutes, never an annual fee.

## Confirmed rebate figures (2026, for reference when doing this as a favour)

- NSW Seniors Energy Rebate: $200/yr (FY26/27 window opens 10 Aug 2026)
- NSW Low Income Household Rebate: ~$285/yr electricity + ~$110/yr gas
- QLD Electricity Rebate: ~$363–399/yr, auto-applies once card registered
