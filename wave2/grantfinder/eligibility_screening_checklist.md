# SME Grant Eligibility — Screening Checklist

Use this as a fast first-pass filter before spending time on a full application
draft. Specific thresholds vary by grant — always check the individual grant's
guidelines before ruling in/out; these are the common recurring criteria to
check first.

## Basic eligibility gate (check first — fastest disqualifiers)

- [ ] ABN active, and active for the minimum period the grant requires (often
      6-12 months, sometimes longer for "established business" grants)
- [ ] GST registered, if the grant requires it
- [ ] Business structure matches what's eligible (sole trader / company /
      partnership / trust — some grants exclude sole traders, note this is
      relevant given the client may be a sole trader like the operator)
- [ ] Turnover within the grant's stated range (many SME grants cap eligible
      turnover, e.g. under $10M, under $2M, etc. — varies a lot by program)
- [ ] Employee count within range, if the grant specifies (e.g. "under 20
      employees" for small business definitions)
- [ ] Industry/sector matches the grant's target (some are industry-specific,
      e.g. manufacturing, agriculture, tourism, tech/innovation)
- [ ] Location matches (some grants are state-specific or regional/postcode
      restricted — check business.gov.au filters and state portals)
- [ ] Purpose of funds matches what the grant will actually pay for (e.g.
      equipment purchase, export development, energy efficiency upgrades,
      digital adoption — read the fine print, generic "for growth" isn't
      enough)

## Financial capacity check

- [ ] Can the business meet any co-contribution requirement (many grants are
      matched funding, e.g. 50/50) — confirm the client can actually fund
      their share
- [ ] Is the business in good financial standing (no outstanding ATO debts,
      no insolvency history) — many grants require this as a condition
- [ ] Does the client have (or can get) a recent set of financials if
      required (tax returns, BAS statements, etc.)

## Compliance/documentation readiness

- [ ] Public liability / relevant insurance in place if required
- [ ] Any required licenses/certifications current
- [ ] Can supply quotes/budgets for the proposed spend if required
- [ ] Available for any required timeline (some grants require project
      completion by a fixed date regardless of when you start)

## Competitive strength check (soft factors, not hard eligibility)

- [ ] Does the application tell a clear, specific story (not generic) about
      why this business needs this specific grant for this specific purpose?
- [ ] Any measurable outcomes to point to (jobs created, revenue growth,
      efficiency gained) — grants assessors weigh concrete outcomes heavily
- [ ] Any letters of support available if the grant values them (industry
      body, local council, customer)

## Outcome of screening

Rate each prospective grant: **Strong fit** (meets all hard criteria, good
story) / **Possible** (meets hard criteria, story needs work) / **Not
eligible** (fails a hard criterion — don't waste time drafting).
