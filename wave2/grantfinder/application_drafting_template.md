# SME Grant Application — Drafting Template

**Scope reminder:** This is a drafting aid to help a client prepare a strong
application. The client submits it themselves (grant portals almost always
require the business's own login/ABN-linked identity) — flag this clearly.
You are not lodging anything on their behalf.

## 1. Business snapshot (reusable across applications, keep a master copy)

- Business name, ABN, structure, date established
- What the business does (1-2 sentences, plain English)
- Current size: revenue (approx.), employee count, locations
- Key achievements to date (2-3 concrete, specific points)

## 2. Project/purpose section (customize per grant)

- What specifically will the grant funds be used for? Be concrete — "new
  CNC machine to increase production capacity by X%" beats "grow the
  business"
- Why now? What's the trigger/opportunity?
- Timeline — start date, key milestones, completion date (must fit the
  grant's required timeframe)
- Budget breakdown — itemized, with quotes attached where required
- Co-contribution — how much the business is putting in, and from where
  (savings, loan, revenue) if a matched-funding grant

## 3. Outcomes section (what assessors weigh most)

- Quantifiable outcomes where possible: jobs created/retained, revenue
  growth expected, efficiency/cost savings, environmental impact, export
  value, etc.
- Community/local benefit if relevant (many state/regional grants weight
  this heavily)
- How outcomes will be measured/reported (some grants require post-project
  reporting — confirm the client understands this ongoing obligation)

## 4. Capability section

- Why this business can deliver the project (relevant experience, existing
  capability, team)
- Any risks and how they'll be managed (assessors like to see this
  considered, not just an optimistic pitch)

## 5. Supporting documents checklist

- [ ] ABN confirmation / business registration
- [ ] Recent financials (as required — tax returns, BAS, management accounts)
- [ ] Quotes/budget for proposed spend
- [ ] Insurance certificates if required
- [ ] Letters of support if relevant/available
- [ ] Any required licenses/certifications

## 6. Final checks before the client submits

- [ ] Every mandatory field/question in the actual grant form addressed
      (don't rely on this generic template's structure matching the form
      exactly — always map back to the real application questions)
- [ ] Word/character limits respected
- [ ] No copy-paste artifacts from other applications (assessors do notice)
- [ ] Client has reviewed and takes ownership of all claims made — you're
      drafting, they're certifying and submitting
