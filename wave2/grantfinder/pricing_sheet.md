# SME Grant-Finder / Rebate-Matching — Pricing Sheet

Curated shortlist + eligibility pre-check report — NOT grant writing (that's
a separate line, `GrantWriting`, still held pending a portfolio).

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | **$79–99 flat (sweet spot $89)** | Curated shortlist + eligibility pre-check, one-off |
| Quick-scan (optional) | **~$49** | Very small/simple businesses |

## Free baseline (confirmed still live, 2026-08-06)

**business.gov.au's own grant finder is free** and still lists ~597 active
federal/state/local programs. The sell here is curation/time-saved, not
access — say so explicitly in marketing copy.

## Competitor evidence

| Competitor | Model | Price | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Grants Hub | Subscription directory | $29/mo or $313/yr (Essential) up to $85/mo or $918/yr (Team) | thegrantshub.com.au/pricing |
| Grant'd | AI grant-matching subscription | $309/yr or $118/quarter | grantd.com.au/plans |
| GrantHelper | Advisory membership (not matching) | $149/yr, or $179 one-off strategy session | granthelper.com.au |
| Fiverr | One-off gig | From ~$46 AUD ("grant research for small business") | fiverr.com search |
| Upwork | Hourly, no packaged product | ~$24-51/hr AU freelancers | Upwork rate pages |

## Reasoning

$89 undercuts every paid AU subscription (cheapest sourced: GrantHelper
$149/yr) with zero lock-in, sits modestly above the lowest Fiverr gig to
reflect AU-specialised curation, and stays cheap to deliver since the
underlying search is free — the fee covers ~1-2hrs of matching/write-up
time.
