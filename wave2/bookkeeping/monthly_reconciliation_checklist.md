# Monthly Reconciliation Checklist (per client)

## Bank & card reconciliation

- [ ] All bank feed transactions imported for the period (no gaps — check
      feed didn't silently disconnect, a common Xero/QBO issue worth checking
      every month)
- [ ] Every transaction categorized (zero "uncategorized" left at month end)
- [ ] Bank rules reviewed — any new recurring transaction pattern this month
      that should get its own rule?
- [ ] Reconciled balance matches actual bank statement closing balance
- [ ] Same for any credit cards / second accounts in scope

## Accuracy checks

- [ ] Spot-check for duplicate transactions (common after a feed reconnect)
- [ ] Spot-check for miscategorized transactions vs prior months (consistency)
- [ ] Check for transactions sitting in a suspense/uncategorized holding
      account — chase the client for clarification on anything unclear
- [ ] Check for personal expenses mixed into business accounts (flag to
      client, don't just categorize silently — this affects their tax
      position and is their accountant's call, not yours)

## Reporting

- [ ] Generate P&L summary for the period
- [ ] Note anything unusual (big one-off expense, revenue spike/drop) in a
      short plain-English cover note — clients rarely read raw P&Ls closely
- [ ] Send to client on the agreed cadence

## BAS/tax-time handoff (when relevant)

- [ ] Confirm all transactions for the BAS period are reconciled and
      categorized BEFORE handoff — do not lodge or advise on BAS content
      yourself (out of scope, no TPB registration)
- [ ] Export/share the reconciled data in the format the client's accountant
      or BAS agent wants
- [ ] Note any transactions you were unsure how to categorize so the
      accountant/BAS agent can make the final call

## Red flags to escalate to the client (not just quietly handle)

- Negative bank balances / bounced payments
- Large unexplained transfers
- Transactions that look like they might be fraud/unauthorized
- Feed disconnections lasting more than a few days (means data gaps)
