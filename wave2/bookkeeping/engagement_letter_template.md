# Bookkeeping Engagement Letter / Scope of Work — Template

**Status:** DRAFT TEMPLATE. Not a legal document and not legally vetted — review
with your own judgement (or a solicitor, if you want it checked) before using
it with a real client, per the standing "review every draft template before
client use" instruction (`PROGRESS.md`'s punch list). Fills Step 5 of
`client_onboarding_checklist.md`, which required a signed engagement letter
but never had one built.

**Software note (2026-08-12):** the business has standardised on **Xero**.
Every reference below to "your accounting software" assumes Xero unless a
client already has an established QBO setup they don't want to move off —
in that case the same letter applies, just swap the platform name.

## [Waters & Co letterhead — see `wave3-unscoped/office_identity/`]

**Bookkeeping Services — Engagement Letter**

Date: [DATE]

Client: [CLIENT LEGAL/BUSINESS NAME]
ABN: [CLIENT ABN]
Contact: [CLIENT CONTACT NAME, EMAIL, PHONE]

Dear [CLIENT NAME],

Thank you for choosing Waters & Co for your bookkeeping. This letter sets out
what's included, what isn't, how we'll work together, and what each of us is
responsible for.

### 1. Scope of services

Waters & Co will:
- Categorise and reconcile your business bank transactions in Xero
- Maintain your chart of accounts
- Set up and periodically review bank rules for recurring transactions
- Provide [monthly/fortnightly — confirm cadence] reporting: [P&L summary /
  reconciliation report — confirm what's agreed]
- Flag unclear or ambiguous transactions to you for clarification

### 2. What is explicitly NOT included

- **BAS lodgement.** Waters & Co is not a registered BAS Agent with the Tax
  Practitioners Board and does not lodge Business Activity Statements, IAS,
  or provide GST/PAYG advice. Your BAS lodgement remains your responsibility
  or your registered agent's — see clause 5.
- Tax advice or tax return preparation of any kind
- Payroll processing (unless separately scoped and added to this agreement)
- Financial product, investment, or superannuation advice

### 3. Fees

| Tier | Monthly transaction volume | Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | Up to 25 transactions/mo | $169/mo |
| Small | 26–100 transactions/mo | From $349/mo |

Agreed tier for this engagement: **[TIER]**, at **$[FEE]/month**, billed
[in advance/in arrears — confirm], due [payment terms — e.g. within 7 days of
invoice].

If your transaction volume regularly exceeds the agreed tier, fees will be
reviewed and discussed with you before any change takes effect — no surprise
increases.

### 4. Access

You (the client) own and pay for your own Xero subscription. You will invite
Waters & Co as a user on your Xero organisation at the access level agreed
below — Waters & Co does not create or pay for your subscription, and does
not ask for more access than the work requires.

- Access level: **[Standard user / Advisor role — confirm which]**
- You are responsible for connecting your own bank feed(s) via your online
  banking login — Waters & Co cannot do this step on your behalf
- You may revoke access at any time; this does not automatically end the
  engagement (see clause 7)

### 5. Responsibilities

**Waters & Co will:**
- Keep your books reconciled and categorised to the agreed cadence
- Communicate promptly about anything unclear or that needs your input
- Hand off a clean set of books to your accountant or registered BAS agent at
  tax/BAS time, in the format and by the date agreed with you

**You will:**
- Respond to requests for clarification on uncategorised/ambiguous
  transactions in a reasonable time
- Maintain your own online banking access and keep the bank feed connected
- Engage and pay your own accountant/registered BAS agent for BAS lodgement,
  tax advice, and tax return preparation — Waters & Co will support that
  handoff but is not responsible for the lodgement itself
- Tell Waters & Co promptly if your accountant or BAS agent changes

### 6. Confidentiality & data

Waters & Co will treat your financial information as confidential and only
use it to provide the services described above. Your data remains in your
own Xero organisation at all times — Waters & Co does not export, store, or
retain a separate copy beyond what's needed to do the work.

### 7. Ending the engagement

Either party may end this engagement with [X weeks — confirm, e.g. 2 weeks]
written notice. On ending, Waters & Co will complete reconciliation up to the
last paid period and hand off cleanly — no books left mid-reconciliation
without notice.

### 8. Acceptance

By signing below, you confirm you understand and agree to the scope, fees,
and responsibilities set out above, including that BAS lodgement and tax
advice are outside this engagement's scope.

## Notes for use (delete before sending to a client)

- Fill every bracketed field before sending — nothing above should reach a
  client with a placeholder still in it.

  agree a clean start date in the scoping call (Step 1 of the onboarding

  silently inherit an unreconciled prior period.

  patch it ad hoc.
