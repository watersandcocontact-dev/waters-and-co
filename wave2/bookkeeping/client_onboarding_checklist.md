# Bookkeeping Client Onboarding Checklist

**Scope reminder:** This service categorizes and reconciles transactions only.
It does NOT include BAS lodgement — that requires registration as a BAS Agent
with the Tax Practitioners Board (TPB), which this business does not have.
Say this explicitly to every prospective client before signing them up, and
in the engagement letter/scope-of-work document.

## Step 1 — Initial scoping call

- [ ] Confirm what accounting software the client uses (or will use) — Xero or
      QBO preferred for bank-feed automation (see `bank_feed_research.md`)
- [ ] Confirm approximate monthly transaction volume
- [ ] Confirm whether they currently have any bookkeeping done, and by whom
      (avoid mid-period handover confusion — clarify a clean start date)
- [ ] Explicitly confirm: "I will categorize and reconcile your transactions,
      but I am not a registered BAS agent, so I won't be lodging your BAS —
      you'll need your accountant or a registered BAS agent for that."
- [ ] Confirm who their accountant is (for handoff at tax time / BAS time)

## Step 2 — Access setup (client does this, not you)

- [ ] Client invites you as a user on THEIR Xero/QBO subscription (you don't
      create or pay for their subscription — flag this clearly: they must sign
      up themselves if they don't already have one)
- [ ] Client connects their bank feed(s) in the software (usually needs their
      own online banking login — you cannot do this step for them)
- [ ] Confirm access level needed (standard user / advisor role — avoid asking
      for more access than the job requires)

## Step 3 — Chart of accounts & rules setup

- [ ] Review/tidy the chart of accounts (or set up a sensible default if new)
- [ ] Set up bank rules for recurring/obvious transactions (see bank-feed
      research doc for how bank rules work per platform)
- [ ] Agree on categorization conventions for ambiguous spend (e.g. how to
      treat mixed personal/business card use, if relevant — flag this as a
      common small-business mess to sort out early)

## Step 4 — Cadence & communication agreement

- [ ] Agree reconciliation frequency (weekly/fortnightly/monthly)
- [ ] Agree how uncategorized/unclear transactions get resolved (e.g. a
      shared list the client answers weekly, vs a call)
- [ ] Agree reporting cadence (e.g. monthly P&L summary)
- [ ] Set the handoff process to their accountant at BAS/tax time (what you
      provide, in what format, by when)

## Step 5 — Engagement letter / scope of work

- [ ] Written engagement letter confirming: scope (categorization +
      reconciliation only, no BAS lodgement, no tax advice), fee, cadence,
      access/security expectations, and a clear line that all lodgements with
      the ATO remain the client's (or their registered agent's) responsibility
- [ ] Client signs before work begins
