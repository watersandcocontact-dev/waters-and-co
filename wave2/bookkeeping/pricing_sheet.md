# Small-Business Bookkeeping (non-BAS) — Pricing Sheet

**BAS lodgement is explicitly out of scope** — that requires TPB
registration, which this business does not hold. Refer BAS work out to a
registered BAS agent.

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Scope |
|---|---|---|

| Small | **$299–449/mo fixed** | 25–100 transactions/mo |

## Complaints/gaps found

- Offshore bookkeeping's low headline hourly rate routinely excludes software licensing, QC/review time, and rework hours — the "cheap" quote isn't the real cost.
- Unpredictable hourly billing is explicitly called out across multiple 2026 AU pricing guides as the reason fixed-fee models are displacing hourly billing.
- MYOB/QuickBooks support complaints (long hold times, unresolved payroll bugs, surprise renewal charges) are a real, positionable gap for a responsive solo operator.

## Reasoning

$149–179/mo undercuts the lowest credible sourced fixed-fee competitor (Advancr's $249/mo) by 30-40% while staying above the "too cheap, must be dodgy" offshore/Fiverr floor (~$98/mo).
