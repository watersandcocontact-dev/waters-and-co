About

Four practices. One point of contact.

Waters & Co is based in Perth — not a call centre, not a large agency with account managers passing you between departments. Whoever you deal with is whoever does the work.

Where we work

Perth in person, Australia-wide (or worldwide) remotely.

In-person work is based out of Doubleview, WA, covering the Perth metro area. Anything that can be delivered remotely — AI systems, bookkeeping, content repurposing, senior tech support over a call — is available Australia-wide, and in some cases worldwide.

When we work

Normal weekday hours, with after-hours by arrangement.

Standard trading hours are weekdays. If a job genuinely needs an after-hours or weekend slot, say so when you get in touch and we'll see what's possible — it's not the default, but it's not off the table either.

Why the four-practice structure

Because "16 services" isn't how anyone actually thinks about their problem.

You bring a problem — a Google listing that's gone stale, a workflow eating your evenings, a parent who needs patient tech help, a valuation that looks wrong. The site is organised around those problems, not around an internal list of what we happen to offer.
